## 4. Convergence, spectral and stability properties｜收敛、谱与稳定性性质

[EN] Resonance spectrum of a PEC square cavity (random initial values, FFT of sampled time series): DGTD with central flux is polluted by non-physical modes; the new method's spectrum is very clear; swapping the subdomains for E and H does not reintroduce spurious modes; M/4 more accurate than M/3 (better mesh quality + more DOFs). L-shaped PEC cavity (singular eigenmodes from the concave feature): no spurious modes, with upwind-flux DGTD as reference. Convergence (TEz11 mode, ten periods, L2 error ⟨公式⟩): optimal rate on both M/3 and M/4; M/4 more accurate; the subdomain assignment matters — better accuracy when subdomains coincident with the initial mesh are used for E (i.e., (4E, XH)). L2(E) > L2(H) explained: H uses the complete first-order polynomial space while Whitney's space for E is incomplete. TM mode study: (XE, 4H) gives lower E-error — for both TE and TM, better accuracy when the subdomains for the transverse electromagnetic component coincide with the initial mesh. Stability: leapfrog requires Δt ≤ 1/ρ(M⁻¹K); PS-DGTD has larger maximum allowable time steps than DGTD (Table 1: e.g., square cavity M/4: 53.2/49.3 ps vs 42.06 ps central-flux DGTD); no significant difference between the two subdomain assignments. Numerical dispersion/dissipation: PS-DGTD has smaller phase-velocity error than upwind DGTD, and its waveform amplitude remains nearly constant (energy conservation), unlike the damped upwind DGTD waveform.

[中] PEC 方腔谐振谱（随机初值、时间序列 FFT）：中心通量 DGTD 的谱被非物理模式污染；新方法的谱非常干净；**E/H 子域互换不重新引入伪模**；M/4 比 M/3 更精确（网格质量更好 + 自由度更多）。L 形 PEC 腔（凹角导致奇异特征模）：同样无伪模（以迎风 DGTD 为参照）。收敛性（TEz11 模、十个周期、L2 误差定义）：M/3 与 M/4 上均为**最优收敛率**；M/4 更精确；子域指派有影响——**E 的子域与初始网格重合（即 (4E, XH)）时精度更好**。L2(E) > L2(H) 的解释：H 用完整的一阶多项式空间而 E 的 Whitney 空间不完备。TM 模式研究：(XE, 4H) 的 E 误差更低——**TE 与 TM 的共同规律：横向电磁分量所在子域与初始网格重合时精度更好**。稳定性：leapfrog 要求 Δt ≤ 1/ρ(M⁻¹K)；PS-DGTD 的最大允许时间步大于 DGTD（表 1：方腔 M/4 上 53.2/49.3 ps 对 42.06 ps）；两种子域指派差异不大。数值色散/耗散：PS-DGTD 相速度误差小于迎风 DGTD，且波形幅值几乎不变（能量守恒），而迎风 DGTD 波形衰减。

## 6. Conclusion｜第6章 结论

[EN] A novel EH-based 2D DGTD method with only central flux is constructed on triangular meshes. To inhibit nonphysical modes, a carefully designed interface condition: on each interface, if the basis functions for E are tangentially continuous then those for H are discontinuous, and vice versa; two independent subdomain sets realize it. Spectrum, convergence, and stability investigated numerically: the method combines spurious-free, optimal convergence, and energy conservation; with second-order leapfrog it has a larger permitted time step than conventional DGTD on the same mesh. Better accuracy when the initial meshes serve as the subdomains for the transverse EM component. Hybrid methods (e.g., combining FDTD with the proposed method) can further enhance efficiency. Extension to 3D and higher orders is ongoing.

[中] 本文在三角网格上构造了**只用中心通量**的新型 EH 型二维 DGTD 方法。为抑制非物理模式，设计了精心策划的界面条件：每个界面上 E 基函数切向连续则 H 不连续，反之亦然；由两组独立子域实现。数值考察了谱、收敛与稳定性：方法兼具无伪模、最优收敛与能量守恒；二阶 leapfrog 推进时，同一网格上允许时间步大于常规 DGTD。横向电磁分量的子域与初始网格重合时精度更佳。混合方法（如与本方法结合的 FDTD）可进一步提升效率。**向三维与高阶的推广正在进行中**。
