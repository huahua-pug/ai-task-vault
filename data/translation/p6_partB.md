## 4. Finite element approximation｜第4章 有限元逼近

[EN] Linear nodal elements on a triangulation; the matrix equation ⟨公式(29)：[K + M + P]U^{n+1} = F^{n+1}⟩ with K (stiffness), M = α²⟨εrφ_i, φ_j⟩ (mass with the Newmark α²), and P^ij = −∫_Γ φ_i(T_αφ_j)dS — the aperture coupling matrix. Off-diagonal (i ≠ j) entries approximated by midpoint quadrature ⟨公式(32)：P_ij ≈ (4 + α²|Γ_i||Γ_j|)/(4π) · K0(α|ξ_i − ξ_j|)⟩; diagonal (i = j) singular integrals approximated via a rapidly convergent series for ∫₀^y K0(τ)dτ ⟨公式(33)⟩. The right-hand side F^{n+1} is recomputed every time step ⟨公式(31)⟩: interior part [M Ũ]; aperture part by midpoint rule including 2∂u^inc/∂y and H̃. H̃ has exponential decay in |r′| but hypersingularity at r′ = (ξ_j, 0): integrated over a truncated rectangle excluding the singular point, partitioned into L×M boxes with midpoint rule ⟨公式(34)⟩ — requires midpoint values of ũ^s in addition to nodal values. The sparsity pattern of [K+M+P] (Fig. 2) shows the FEM band structure plus the dense coupling block between the two apertures induced by T_α.

[中] 三角剖分上线性节点元；矩阵方程 ⟨公式(29)：[K + M + P]U^{n+1} = F^{n+1}⟩：K 刚度阵、M 含 Newmark α² 的质量阵、P 为**口面耦合矩阵** P^ij = −∫_Γ φ_i(T_αφ_j)dS。非对角元（i≠j）用中点求积 ⟨公式(32)⟩；对角元奇异积分用收敛很快的 K0 积分级数展开 ⟨公式(33)⟩。右端 F^{n+1} 每个时间步重算 ⟨公式(31)⟩：体内部分为 [M Ũ]，口面部分用中点公式计入 2∂u^inc/∂y 与 H̃。H̃ 的被积函数随 |r′| 指数衰减但在 (ξ_j,0) 处超奇异：在挖去奇异点的截断矩形域上积分，按 L×M 个矩形中点求积 ⟨公式(34)⟩——除节点值外还需 ũ^s 的中点值。矩阵 [K+M+P] 的稀疏模式（图 2）呈现 FEM 带状结构加上 T_α 诱导的**两口面之间的稠密耦合块**。

## 5. Numerical examples｜第5章 数值算例

[EN] Two 1 m × 0.25 m rectangular cavities spaced 1 m apart; uniform triangular mesh r = 0.05 m; Newmark h = 0.05 light-meters, γ = 0.9, β = 0.25(0.5+γ)²; normal incidence. (1) Continuous wave (λ = 1 m): after ~20 cycles transients propagate out leaving the periodic solution, consistent with published single-cavity results [7]; at one-wavelength separation the coupling effect on aperture solutions is minimal. To isolate coupling: define the normalized influence factor ⟨公式：C_εr(d,M) = max_{x∈Γ₁₁, t∈[20,T]} |E²_εr(d,M) − E¹_εr| / M⟩ comparing two-cavity responses against single-cavity references — the influence decays exponentially with cavity distance, tracking multiples of e^{−4d}. (2) Gaussian pulse: cavity 1 unfilled (εr = 1), cavity 2 filled (εr = 2); spectral content shows the primary resonance spike at 1/L for εr = 2 (consistent with the round-trip propagation time); even at 0.05 m separation the coupling is dominated by the primary scattering — subtracting single-cavity responses isolates the coupling effect (spectral content of the influence shown); oblique incidence θ^inc = π/6 with 0.05 m separation demonstrates aperture fields and cross-talk similarly.

[中] 几何：两个 1 m × 0.25 m 矩形腔、间距 1 m；均匀三角网格 r = 0.05 m；Newmark 参数 h = 0.05 光米、γ = 0.9、β = 0.25(0.5+γ)²；法向入射。(1) **连续波**（λ = 1 m）：约 20 个周期后瞬态扰动传出，留下周期解，与已发表单腔结果 [7] 一致；间距为一个波长时耦合对口面解的影响很小。为分离耦合效应，定义**归一化影响因子** C_εr(d,M)（双腔解与单腔参考解之差的最大值归一化）：影响随腔间距**指数衰减**，与 e^{−4d} 的倍数吻合。(2) **高斯脉冲**：腔 1 空（εr = 1）、腔 2 填充（εr = 2）；频谱 content 显示 εr = 2 时在 1/L 处出现主谐振尖峰（与传播往返时间一致）；即便间距仅 0.05 m，耦合仍被主散射主导——把单腔解相减即可分离串扰（给出影响的频谱）；斜入射 θ^inc = π/6、间距 0.05 m 的算例同样展示了口面场与串扰。

## 6. Conclusion｜第6章 结论

[EN] This paper presents an application of the FE-BI method utilizing the Newmark method to model transient scattering from two cavities embedded in a ground plane, extending [7] (single-cavity time domain) and [8] (multiple-cavity frequency domain). The time-discretized problem is solved by FE-BI; the variational formulation is shown to be well-posed for each discrete time step and the numerical implementation with rectangular cavities demonstrates stability. Future research will investigate effects of spacing, depth, and additional cavities — primarily how adjusting distance and depth may cause constructive or destructive coupling between cavities.

[中] 本文给出了采用 Newmark 方法的有限元-边界积分方法在接地平面双腔瞬态散射上的应用，把 [7]（单腔时域）与 [8]（多腔频域）的工作向前推进。时间离散后的问题用 FE-BI 求解；变分形式对每个离散时间步适定，矩形腔的数值实现展示了稳定性。未来研究将考察间距、深度与更多腔体的影响——核心关注点是如何通过调节距离与深度实现腔间耦合的相长或相消设计。
