# 求解Maxwell方程的单向区域分解方法与快速GMRES求解器（英中对照·分片1：摘要、引言、表述与离散）

体例：[EN] 原文（公式用字体安全 Unicode），[中] 汉译。

## Abstract｜摘要

[EN] For the solution of the time-harmonic electromagnetic scattering problem by inhomogeneous 3-D objects, a one-way domain decomposition method (DDM) is considered: the computational domain is partitioned into concentric subdomains on the interfaces of which Robin-type transmission conditions (TCs) are prescribed; an integral representation of the electromagnetic fields on the outer boundary constitutes an exact radiation condition. The global system obtained after discretization of the finite element (FE) formulations is solved via a Krylov subspace iterative method (GMRES), preconditioned so that essentially only the solution of the FE subsystems in each subdomain is required. This is made possible by a computationally cheap H(curl)–H(div) transformation on the interfaces. The eigenvalues of the preconditioned matrix are bounded by two; optimized values of the local TC coefficients maximize the minimum eigenvalue. Numerical experiments illustrate the accuracy, the fast convergence, and legitimate the optimized coefficients.

[中] 针对非均匀三维物体的时谐电磁散射问题，考虑一种**单向区域分解方法（DDM）**：把计算域剖分为同心子域，在交界面上给 Robin 型传输条件（TC）；外边界上电磁场的积分表示构成**精确辐射条件**。有限元（FE）表述离散后的全局系统用 Krylov 子空间迭代法（GMRES）求解，预条件子的设计使得**本质上只需逐个子域求解 FE 子系统**——这得益于交界面上计算廉价的 H(curl)–H(div) 变换。预条件矩阵的特征值被 2 所界；通过极大化最小特征值确定局部 TC 系数的优化值。数值实验展示了方法的精度与快速收敛，并佐证了优化系数的选取。

## 1. Introduction｜引言

[EN] Scattering by penetrable complex 3-D objects is accurately solved by hybrid finite element / integral equation (FE-IE) methods: a surface Sf encloses the inhomogeneous medium as the outer boundary of the FE computational domain Ω. The coupled FE-IE system — sparse FE matrix + dense IE matrix — is computationally intensive for electrically large objects. A domain decomposition method (DDM) splits the large problem into coupled subproblems; resources are further reduced if an absorbing boundary condition (ABC) is prescribed on Sf, but an ABC is not accurate enough for stealth objects (large RCS angular dynamic range). Hybrid DDM-IE techniques couple the IE via a Robin transmission condition (TC): the global system with a costly dense-IE preconditioner; a relaxed Gauss–Seidel (GS) on concentric subdomains (slow convergence, but each FE subproblem solved by an efficient parallel direct solver); the IE solved independently at each GS iteration (costly mat-vecs). Here the GS is replaced by a preconditioned GMRES (DDM-IR-GMRES): the integral representations (IR) of E and H relate their values on Sf to those on the inner boundary S of the outermost subdomain — the IR constitute an exact radiation condition and the Maxwell problem is uniquely solvable.

[中] 含复杂介质的三维物体散射常用 FE-IE 混合方法精确求解：曲面 Sf 包围非均匀介质、作为 FE 计算域 Ω 的外边界。FE-IE 耦合系统（稀疏 FE 矩阵 + 稠密 IE 矩阵）在电大尺寸时计算代价高。区域分解方法（DDM）把大问题拆成若干耦合子问题；若 Sf 上用吸收边界条件（ABC）可进一步省资源，但对隐身目标（RCS 角域动态范围极大）ABC 精度不够。为此有混合 DDM-IE 技术（Robin 传输条件耦合 IE）：或对全局系统做稠密 IE 矩阵的昂贵预条件；或在同心子域上用松弛 Gauss–Seidel（GS）迭代（收敛可能慢，但每个 FE 子问题可用高效并行直接解法）；IE 在每次 GS 迭代中独立求解（矩阵-向量积过多）。本文用**预条件 GMRES 取代 GS（DDM-IR-GMRES）**：用电磁场的积分表示（IR）把 Sf 上的场值与最外子域内边界 S 上的值联系起来——IR 构成**精确辐射条件**，相应 Maxwell 问题解唯一。

[EN] After discretization, the global system Ax = b is preconditioned by the block-diagonal matrix M of the diagonal blocks of A: M involves only the invertible FE matrices A_i of each subdomain and well-conditioned real Gram matrices on the interfaces — the preconditioning cost is essentially the solution of subsystems A_i x_i = b_i. The eigenvalues of M⁻¹A lie in the disk centered at 1 with radius strictly smaller than 1. The curl2div (H(curl)→H(div)) transformation allows accurate discretization of the IR. High-order TCs (HOTC) could a priori converge faster, but no theory guarantees well-posedness of the subproblems for all HOTCs, and some HOTCs require additional interface unknowns. Outline: Section 2 formulation and Ax=b; Section 3 optimized TC coefficients and GMRES convergence rate for a planar analytical case; Section 4 numerical experiments; Section 5 conclusions.

[中] 离散后得到全局系统 Ax = b，用 A 的对角块构成的块对角矩阵 M 做预条件：M 只含各子域可逆的 FE 矩阵 A_i 与交界面上良态的实 Gram 矩阵——预条件成本本质上是求解子系统 A_i x_i = b_i。**可严格证明 M⁻¹A 的特征值位于以 1 为心、半径严格小于 1 的圆盘内**。curl2div（H(curl)→H(div)）变换保证 IR 的精确离散。高阶传输条件（HOTC）虽然原则上收敛更快，但缺乏对所有 HOTC 保证子问题适定性的理论，且部分 HOTC 需要在交界面上引入附加未知量、增加子问题求解复杂度——本文坚持低阶严格局部 Robin TC。结构：第 2 章表述与 Ax=b；第 3 章以平面解析情形讨论 TC 优化系数与 GMRES 收敛率；第 4 章数值实验；第 5 章结论。
