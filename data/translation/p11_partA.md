# 具有精确能量与电荷守恒的半隐式电磁FEM-PIC格式（英中对照·分片1：摘要、引言、框架、参考格式）

体例：[EN] 原文（公式用字体安全 Unicode），[中] 汉译。

## Abstract｜摘要

[EN] This article presents a new charge- and energy-conserving semi-implicit method (ChECSIM) for the Vlasov-Maxwell system. The Particle-in-Cell space discretization involves a structure-preserving Finite Element Method for the fields; the time discretization adapts Lapenta's energy-conserving semi-implicit method (ECSIM), essentially by improving it with appropriate charge-conserving current deposition terms and an explicit correction step. The resulting scheme is free of nonlinear iterations, and preserves both energy and charge exactly.

[中] 本文提出 Vlasov-Maxwell 系统的一种新的**电荷与能量同时守恒的半隐式方法（ChECSIM）**。Particle-in-Cell（PIC）空间离散对场采用**保结构有限元方法**；时间离散是对 Lapenta 的能量守恒半隐式方法（ECSIM）的改造——核心是用恰当的**电荷守恒电流沉积项**与一个**显式校正步**完善之。所得格式**不含任何非线性迭代**，且**同时精确守恒能量与电荷**。

## 1. Introduction｜引言

[EN] Long-time accuracy relies on preserving fundamental invariants (total energy) at the discrete level; for EM particle solvers, Gauss's laws are also key invariants — spurious virtual charges build up otherwise. Continuously, Gauss laws hinge on (i) the continuity equation ∂tρ + div J = 0 for the sources and (ii) div curl = 0 — now understood via the de Rham structure. In PIC methods, charge-preserving solvers divide into field correction methods vs conservative deposition methods; both require Maxwell solvers preserving the de Rham structure (structure-preserving methods). Recent PIC codes preserving exactly the total energy: Markidis–Lapenta's implicit energy-conserving method (nonlinear Newton steps, particle enslavement reformulating nonlinear problems on the fields only; Gauss laws approximately via pseudo-current corrections); Chen–Chacón–Barnes go further with charge-conserving deposition via particle enslavement (exact Gauss laws even without full Newton convergence). Classical explicit leapfrog + charge-conserving deposition preserves Gauss laws but only a modified discrete energy, with CFL constraints (~cell light propagation time). Energy-conserving schemes allow much larger time steps, but both above have fully nonlinear implicit steps.

[中] 数值模拟的长时间精度依赖于在离散层面保持基本不变量（总能量）；对电磁粒子求解器，**Gauss 定律同样是关键不变量**——否则虚拟电荷不断累积。连续层面 Gauss 定律的保持依赖两点：(i) 源满足连续性方程 ∂tρ + div J = 0；(ii) div curl ≡ 0——如今在 **de Rham 结构**框架下被充分理解。PIC 方法中的电荷守恒求解器分两类：场校正法与守恒沉积法；两者都要求 Maxwell 求解器保持 de Rham 结构（即**保结构方法**）。近期精确守恒总能量的 PIC 代码：Markidis–Lapenta 的隐式能量守恒法（非线性 Newton 步、"粒子奴役"技术把非线性问题只 reformulate 到场上；Gauss 定律靠伪电流校正近似满足）；Chen–Chacón–Barnes 进一步用粒子奴役实现电荷守恒沉积（即使 Newton 不完全收敛也精确保持 Gauss 定律）。经典显式 leapfrog + 电荷守恒沉积保持 Gauss 定律但只保持**修正离散能量**，且受 CFL 约束（约等于光波单元传播时间）。能量守恒格式可用大得多的时间步，但上述两法的隐式步**完全非线性**。

[EN] This limitation is removed by Lapenta's Energy-Conserving Semi-Implicit Method (ECSIM): particle enslavement applied at the time-stepping level makes the implicit problems not only reduced to field unknowns but also **linear**. A drawback: a priori not compatible with charge-conserving current deposition — the latter involves time-averages along particle trajectories, not expressible as a linear function of the electric field. Remedies: local linearization of particle shapes [17] (recursive Gauss error reduction); geometric/structure-preserving FEM-PIC [30] (exact energy + Gauss laws but a new nonlinear problem). This article: a new method with semi-implicit time stepping free of nonlinear iterations, preserving both energy and Gauss laws exactly — strategy: endow ECSIM with a semi-conservative current deposition + an explicit correction completing the step with a fully charge-conserving deposition + a velocity rescaling restoring energy conservation. Cost close to ECSIM plus a conservative deposition (similar to explicit charge-conserving leapfrog [11]); naturally formulated in the structure-preserving FEM framework [14,43] — allowing unstructured meshes, complex geometries, local adaptations. Motivating application: vacuum electronic devices (Klystron cavities etc.) — reduced models struggle with troublemaking phenomena; demand for complete Maxwell-Vlasov solvers with stable schemes free of CFL and affordable complexity.

[中] Lapenta 的 **ECSIM** 移除了这一限制：把"粒子奴役"用在时间推进这一层，使隐式问题不仅只含场未知量、而且是**线性**的。缺点：先验上**与电荷守恒沉积不兼容**——后者需要沿粒子轨迹的时间平均，无法写成电场的线性函数。已有补救：粒子形状局部线性化的位置校正 [17]（递归减小 Gauss 误差）；几何/保结构 FEM-PIC 框架 [30]（能量与 Gauss 全精确，但引入新的非线性问题）。本文提出新方法：半隐式时间推进**无任何非线性迭代**、能量与 Gauss 定律**同时精确守恒**——策略：先给 ECSIM 配上半守恒电流沉积，再加显式校正步以完整的电荷守恒沉积完成时间步，最后做**速度重标定**恢复能量守恒。成本接近 ECSIM 加一次守恒沉积（类似显式电荷守恒 leapfrog [11]）；且天然落于保结构有限元框架 [14,43]——支持非结构网格、复杂几何、局部自适应。应用动机：真空电子器件工业场景（速调管谐振腔等）——缩减模型难以捕获致障现象，业界需要**完整 Maxwell-Vlasov 模型**的无 CFL 稳定格式。

## 4. Two reference FEM-PIC schemes｜两个参考格式

[EN] 4.1 CCLF (charge-conserving leapfrog [11]): staggered particle push ⟨公式(40)⟩; staggered Maxwell ⟨公式(41)(43)⟩; time-averaged current source ⟨公式(42)(44)：j^{n+1/2} = Σ q_k v^{n+1/2}_k{Λ¹_i}^n_k with {g}^n_k = (1/Δt)∫ g(x_k(t))dt⟩ — constant velocity on the step allows placement inside/outside the average. Proposition 4.1: CCLF preserves the discrete Gauss laws ⟨公式(45)(46)⟩ — the time-averaged current gives a proper discrete continuity equation ⟨公式(47)⟩. But it does not preserve the energy ⟨公式(48)⟩ (source-free: only a pseudo-energy, controlled under CFL). 4.2 ECSIM (Lapenta [33], FEM version à la [43]): particle push with midpoint velocity and half-step-averaged field ⟨公式(49)⟩; implicit Maxwell ⟨公式(50)(51)⟩ with point-valued current at midpoints. Key: the velocity kick rewrites as v^{n+1/2} = A^n_k(v^n_k + η_k E^{n+1/2}_h) with A^n_k = (I − η_k B^n_k)⁻¹ ⟨公式(52)⟩ — decomposing the current into explicit + implicit parts ⟨公式(53)(54)：j_imp = (Δt/2)L^n(e^n + e^{n+1/2})⟩ with the **Lapenta matrix** ⟨公式(55)：L^n = Σ (q²_k/m_k) A^n_k Λ¹_j(x)·Λ¹_i(x)⟩, yielding the linearly-implicit Maxwell system ⟨公式(56)⟩. Proposition 4.2: ECSIM = (i) push to n+1/2, (ii) explicit current + Lapenta matrix, (iii) solve linear Maxwell system, (iv) velocity update, (v) push to n+1. Proposition 4.3: ECSIM preserves the discrete energy (48) — the three energy contributions cancel ⟨公式(57)(58)⟩. Proposition 4.4: the linear system (56) is always invertible (Appendix A).

[中] 4.1 **CCLF（电荷守恒 leapfrog [11]）**：交错粒子推进入 ⟨公式(40)⟩；交错 Maxwell ⟨公式(41)(43)⟩；**时间平均电流源** ⟨公式(42)(44)⟩——步内速度恒定使其可放进/提出时间平均。**命题 4.1**：CCLF 保持离散 Gauss 定律 ⟨公式(45)(46)⟩——时间平均电流给出恰当的离散连续性方程 ⟨公式(47)⟩。但**不保持能量** ⟨公式(48)⟩（无源时只保持一个伪能量，需 CFL 控制才有界）。4.2 **ECSIM（Lapenta [33] 的 FEM 版）**：粒子推进用中点速度与半步平均场 ⟨公式(49)⟩；隐式 Maxwell ⟨公式(50)(51)⟩，电流在粒子中点取点值。关键改写：速度半步更新可写成 v^{n+1/2} = A^n_k(v^n_k + η_k E^{n+1/2}_h)，A^n_k = (I − η_k B^n_k)⁻¹ ⟨公式(52)⟩——电流分解为显式 + 隐式两部分 ⟨公式(53)(54)⟩，隐式部分含 **Lapenta 矩阵** ⟨公式(55)⟩，得到**线性隐式** Maxwell 系统 ⟨公式(56)⟩。**命题 4.2**：ECSIM 等价于五步流程（推半步→显式电流+Lapenta 矩阵→解线性 Maxwell 系统→速度更新→推后半步）。**命题 4.3**：ECSIM 精确保持离散能量 (48)——场能、磁能、动能三项贡献相消 ⟨公式(57)(58)⟩。**命题 4.4**：线性系统 (56) 恒可逆（附录 A）。
