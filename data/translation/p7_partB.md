## 4. Error analysis and stability｜误差分析与稳定性

[EN] Discrete balance: choosing constant weights recovers the balance laws ⟨公式(46)⟩ **at the element level** (a consequence of discontinuous bases, impossible with continuous FEM) and with target fluxes M* (physically correct fluxes preserving the characteristic structure). Energy dissipation ⟨公式(47)：Δ_Q = −∫_∂Q N(JU^Kh)⟩: the dissipation equals the boundary energy flux of the difference between target and interior traces, and is **always nonnegative**: ⟨公式(48)：Δ = Σ_Q Δ_Q ≥ 0⟩ — the method is dissipative and energetically stable (numerically verified in §6.1.1). Fronts and layers on causal meshes: partial ordering of patches; layers S_k = {Q : ∂Q^ci ⊂ F_{k−1}}, fronts F_k ⟨公式(49)(50)⟩. Norms: full spacetime norm ⟨公式(51)：‖U‖²_{e,Q} = ∫_Q [E·E + H·H + ½(E·D + H·B)]Ω⟩ (L² + energy) and front norm ⟨公式(52)⟩. Error indicators ⟨公式(53)：ψ = ψ_Ω + ψ_F⟩ — stringent (needs exact solution); the practical indicator is the energy dissipation Δ.

[中] 离散平衡：取常值权函数即**在单元层面**恢复平衡律 ⟨公式(46)⟩——这是间断基函数的结果（连续 FEM 做不到），且平衡律中使用目标通量 M*（保持问题特征结构的物理正确通量）。能量耗散 ⟨公式(47)⟩：单元耗散等于"目标通量与内部迹之差"造成的边界能流，且**恒非负**：⟨公式(48)：Δ = Σ_Q Δ_Q ≥ 0⟩——方法耗散、能量稳定（§6.1.1 数值验证）。因果网格上的层与波前：patch 偏序、层 S_k 与波前 F_k 的递推 ⟨公式(49)(50)⟩。范数：全时空范数 ⟨公式(51)⟩（L² + 能量）与波前范数 ⟨公式(52)⟩。误差指标 ⟨公式(53)：ψ = ψ_Ω + ψ_F⟩——严格但需精确解；实践中的指标是能量耗散 Δ。

## 5. Implementation｜实现

[EN] Causality constraint: a face is causal if it is "faster" (closer to horizontal) than the fastest characteristic directions; a causal mesh enables asymmetric dependencies (element A depends only on earlier neighbors B, C) → local element-by-element asynchronous solution, linear complexity in the number of spacetime elements. Tent-Pitcher algorithm ⟨Fig. 8⟩: patches of tetrahedra erected around a vertex advanced in local time; interleaved pitching and solving until D is filled; causality restricts the vertex time advance.

[中] **因果性约束**：若一个面比最快特征方向更"平"（更接近水平），则称其为因果面；因果网格使相邻单元的依赖关系不对称（单元 A 只依赖更早的邻居 B、C）→ 逐单元局部异步求解，复杂度随时空单元数线性。Tent-Pitcher 算法 ⟨图8⟩：以局部时间坐标抬升顶点、生成一 patch 四面体单元；"竖帐篷—解 patch—更新波前"交替进行直到填满 D；因果性约束限制顶点的时间推进量。

## 6. Numerical results｜数值结果

[EN] 6.1.1 Convergence (smooth solution ⟨公式(54)⟩, unit cube, d = 1–3, p = 0–6/5/3): energy dissipation Δ converges at rate ≈ **2p + 1** (higher than 2p − 1 for elastodynamics [21] because fields are directly interpolated, not derived from displacement); the stringent indicator ψ converges at the optimal **2p + 2**. 6.1.2 Von Neumann analysis (1D, k = 5π): dispersion error Δω_R (early/late arrival) and dissipation Δω_I (amplitude loss) extracted from the numerical maximum position/value ⟨公式(55)-(57)⟩; waves arrive late for p = 0; both errors vanish with refinement — observed rates **p** (dispersion) and **p + 1** (dissipation); analytic derivation planned as future work. 6.2 Nonsmooth 1D step discontinuity (two materials, ε_r = 1 vs 10): h-refinement steepens the jump but overshoot magnitude is relatively insensitive to h; p-enrichment is overly dissipative for p = 1 and oscillatory for larger p; convergence rate of Δ and ψ ≈ 1 — **governed by solution regularity (L² only), not polynomial order**. 6.3 2D problems resolve sharp moving wave fronts (e.g., circular ring problem under Silver–Müller BC, Fig. 18: E₃ and Poynting magnitude).

[中] 6.1.1 收敛性（光滑解 ⟨公式(54)⟩，单位立方，d = 1–3，p = 0–6/5/3）：能量耗散 Δ 收敛阶 ≈ **2p + 1**（高于弹性动力学 [21] 的 2p − 1——因为电磁场被直接插值而非由位移求导）；严格指标 ψ 达到最优阶 **2p + 2**。6.1.2 von Neumann 分析（1D，k = 5π）：由数值极大值的位置/幅值提取色散误差 Δω_R（超前/滞后）与耗散误差 Δω_I ⟨公式(55)-(57)⟩；p = 0 时波到达偏晚；两种误差随加密消失——观察到的收敛阶为 **p**（色散）与 **p + 1**（耗散）；解析推导列为未来工作。6.2 一维阶梯间断（双材料 ε_r = 1 与 10）：h 加密使跳跃更陡但过冲幅度对 h 不敏感；p 加密在 p = 1 时过度耗散、p 更大时呈小幅振荡；Δ 与 ψ 的收敛阶 ≈ 1——**受解正则性（仅 L²）而非多项式阶支配**。6.3 二维算例分辨尖锐运动波前（如 Silver–Müller 边界下的圆环问题，图 18：E₃ 与坡印廷矢量幅值）。

## 7. Conclusions｜第7章 结论

[EN] Modern remote sensing problems are defined on complex geometries/media, inherently multiscale and multiphysics; some situations (e.g., turbulent flow) are truly spacetime in character and require a genuine spacetime approach. The aSDG method for linear electromagnetics: directly discretizes space and time → high temporal order, linear cost scaling; differential forms yield the compact spacetime flux density M, expressing all four Maxwell equations and balance laws for arbitrary spacetime domains, and avoiding the problematic spacetime normal vector; DG discretization of Faraday and Ampère–Maxwell laws, satisfied at the element level in the discrete setting; energy is not balanced — the method is dissipative (energetically stable). Patch-wise causal meshes give asynchronous local solution with linear complexity; time advance of elements is independent of interpolation order, limited only by causality. Convergence studies: energy dissipation rate 2p + 1; ψ indicator 2p + 2; von Neumann dispersion/dissipation rates p and p + 1; nonsmooth problems regularity-limited; 2D simulations resolve sharp fronts.

[中] 现代遥感问题几何与介质复杂、本质多尺度多物理；有些情形（如湍流）是"真时空"问题，必须用真正的时空方法。本文线性电磁的 aSDG 方法：时空直接离散 → 高时间阶 + 线性成本；微分形式给出紧凑的时空通量密度 M，一条式子表达全部四条 Maxwell 方程与任意时空域上的平衡律，并**回避了有问题的时空法向量定义**；对 Faraday 与 Ampère–Maxwell 定律作 DG 离散，离散层面在单元级满足；能量不严格平衡——方法是耗散的（能量稳定）。patch 级因果网格带来异步局部求解与线性复杂度；单元时间推进与插值阶无关、只受因果性限制。收敛研究：能量耗散阶 2p + 1、ψ 指标 2p + 2、von Neumann 色散/耗散阶 p 与 p + 1；非光滑问题受正则性限制；二维算例分辨尖锐波前。
