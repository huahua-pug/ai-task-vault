# 英中对照·分片2：稳定性分析、数值实验与结论

## 3. Numerical experiments｜第3章 数值实验

[EN] The Curl system has a large null space and becomes numerically unstable with nearly-zero air conductivity — no successful attempt of applying edge-based FEM to 2D MT was reported. The air-Earth decomposition overcomes this via the Laplace operator in the air. Models: COMMEMI 2D-1 benchmark (conductive rectangle 0.5 Ω·m in 100 Ω·m half-space); land topography (sinusoidal hill/valley, 100 Ω·m); seafloor bathymetry (MARE2DEM model, seawater 0.3 Ω·m, depth 936-2190 m); realistic mineralization (Kiruna iron ore, two dipping zones 1/0.5 Ω·m in 10000 Ω·m, weathered layer 100 Ω·m). Triangle meshing; PARDISO direct solver; i7 CPU, 16 GB RAM.

[中] Curl 系统零空间巨大、在空气电导率近零时数值不稳定——此前没有棱边元 FEM 求解二维 MT 的成功报道。空-地分解借助空气域的拉普拉斯算子克服之。模型：COMMEMI 2D-1 基准（100 Ω·m 半空间中 0.5 Ω·m 导电矩形体）；陆地地形（正弦山/谷，100 Ω·m）；海底地形（MARE2DEM 模型，海水 0.3 Ω·m，水深 936–2190 m）；实际矿化模型（瑞典 Kiruna 铁矿，10000 Ω·m 背景中两条倾斜矿化带 1/0.5 Ω·m，风化层 100 Ω·m）。Triangle 剖分；PARDISO 直接解法器；i7 CPU、16 GB 内存。

[EN] 3.2 Stability: Curl-system obstacles — (1) low-frequency limit: the curl-curl term dominates over iωμσE, any gradient field ∇φ lies in the null space (∇×α∇×(u+∇φ) = 0) → ill-conditioned over the whole domain; (2) nearly-zero air conductivity → ill-posed in Ω₁₂; worse for the magnetic curl-curl equation whose first term ∇×σ⁻¹∇×H becomes indefinite in the air (TE unstable, frequency- and air-conductivity-dependent). The hybrid system: curl-curl only in the Earth (σ > 0 → well-posed), Helmholtz (Laplace operator: trivial null space, elliptic) in the air → overcomes the near-zero-conductivity problem and improves low-frequency stability. Stability tests (2D-1, condition numbers): vs mesh refinement — all increase, hybrid significantly smaller than Curl; vs air conductivity (10⁻⁶ to 10⁻¹⁶ S/m) — hybrid nearly unaffected, magnetic Curl increases significantly (∇×σ⁻¹∇×H nearly singular in air); vs frequency (10³ to 10⁻³ Hz) — frequency decrease enlarges the curl-curl null space, hybrid still significantly smaller.

[中] 3.2 稳定性：Curl 系统两大障碍——(1) **低频极限**：curl-curl 项远大于 iωμσE 项，任意梯度场 ∇φ 都在其零空间（∇×α∇×(u+∇φ) = 0）→ 全域病态；(2) **空气电导率近零** → Ω₁₂ 内不适定；磁场 curl-curl 方程更糟——其第一项 ∇×σ⁻¹∇×H 在空气中**不定**，TE 模式不稳定且依赖频率与空气电导率取值。混合系统：curl-curl 只在大地域（σ > 0 → 适定），空气中为 Helmholtz（拉普拉斯算子：平凡零空间、椭圆性质）→ 克服近零电导率问题、改善低频稳定性。稳定性测试（2D-1 模型、条件数）：随网格加密——三者条件数都增大，但混合系统显著小于 Curl；随空气电导率（10⁻⁶–10⁻¹⁶ S/m）——混合系统几乎不受影响，磁场 Curl 系统显著增大（∇×σ⁻¹∇×H 在空气中近奇异）；随频率（10³–10⁻³ Hz）——频率降低扩大 curl-curl 零空间，混合系统条件数仍显著更小。

[EN] Convergence (QMR + ILU, 2D-1, 10–0.01 Hz): (1) Helmholtz fastest; (2) hybrid significantly improved vs Curl; (3) lower frequency → more iterations; (4) magnetic Curl-curl convergence worsens as air conductivity drops from 10⁻⁸ to 10⁻¹⁴ S/m (singularity in air). Dependency tests (102,599 elements, 10 Hz & 0.1 Hz): Curl fails to reach QMR tolerance → PARDISO used; at air σ = 10⁻¹⁰ S/m the Curl TE mode shows large errors at 0.1 Hz (frequency dependence); at σ = 10⁻⁸ S/m the Curl TE mode is correct even at 0.1 Hz (air-conductivity dependence). Timing (0.1 Hz, 263k nodes / 787k edges): hybrid assembles slower than Curl but has fewer unknowns and shorter solve time (5.424 vs 5.859 s; Helmholtz 2.573 s).

[中] 收敛性（QMR + ILU、2D-1、10–0.01 Hz）：(1) Helmholtz 最快；(2) 混合系统显著优于 Curl；(3) 频率越低迭代越多；(4) 空气电导率从 10⁻⁸ 降到 10⁻¹⁴ S/m 时磁场 Curl-curl 收敛性越来越差（空气中奇异性）。依赖性测试（102,599 单元、10 Hz 与 0.1 Hz）：Curl 系统无法达到 QMR 容差 → 改用 PARDISO 直接解法器；空气 σ = 10⁻¹⁰ S/m 时 Curl 的 TE 模式在 0.1 Hz 出现大误差（**频率依赖**）；σ = 10⁻⁸ S/m 时 0.1 Hz 也正确（**空气电导率依赖**）。耗时对比（0.1 Hz、26.3 万节点/78.7 万棱边）：混合系统组装慢于 Curl，但未知量更少、求解更快（5.424 对 5.859 s；Helmholtz 2.573 s）。

[EN] Applications: (1) Land topography model (457,504 elements, air σ = 10⁻¹⁰ S/m where Curl fails; 1/0.1/0.01 Hz; 99 sites): good agreement between Helmholtz and hybrid systems; topographic effects weaken as frequency decreases; TM mode more affected by topography. (2) Seafloor bathymetry (MARE2DEM model; 16 frequencies 10⁻⁴–0.1 Hz; 407,205 elements): apparent resistivities agree with MARE2DEM for both TE and TM modes. (3) Mineralization model (Kiruna; 33 frequencies 1 Hz–10 kHz; 246,688 elements; 31 sites): excellent agreement with the Helmholtz system — the hybrid system handles realistic problems.

[中] 应用算例：(1) **陆地地形模型**（457,504 单元，空气 σ = 10⁻¹⁰ S/m——Curl 系统在此无法正确求解；频点 1/0.1/0.01 Hz；99 个测点）：Helmholtz 与混合系统吻合良好；地形影响随频率降低而减弱；TM 模式受地形影响更大。(2) **海底地形模型**（MARE2DEM 模型；16 个频点 10⁻⁴–0.1 Hz；407,205 单元）：TE/TM 两模式视电阻率均与 MARE2DEM 吻合。(3) **矿化模型**（Kiruna；33 个频点 1 Hz–10 kHz；246,688 单元；31 个测点）：与 Helmholtz 系统解高度一致——混合系统可处理实际问题。

## 4. Discussion and conclusions｜第4章 讨论与结论

[EN] A new alternative working algorithm (hybrid Helmholtz-curl formulae system) for 2D MT: air field governed by the well-posed Helmholtz equation (stable even at low frequency); Earth field by the well-posed curl-curl equation. Considering the different physical properties of air and Earth, the hybrid system is numerically stable: condition numbers nearly independent of air conductivity and significantly smaller than the Curl system; the Curl system is unstable at low frequency and its correctness depends heavily on air conductivity. Applications (land topography, seafloor bathymetry, mineralization) show it is an alternative algorithm for 2D MT forward solvers. Conventionally, curl-curl stability in air at low frequency is improved by explicitly enforcing divergence-free conditions via Lagrange multipliers; here the improvement comes from the well-posed Helmholtz equation in the air. Due to overcoming the air-instability at low frequency, the hybrid system may resolve the well-known outstanding numerical instability in 3D MT forward modeling. The 3D derivations are less convenient than 2D (no TE/TM decomposition) but the procedures are almost the same: (1) a stable vector Helmholtz equation for air and a stable curl-curl equation for Earth; (2) hybrid FE discretization with special care on the air-Earth interface continuity. Generalization to 3D geoelectromagnetic problems will be discussed in future work.

[中] 本文提出二维 MT 的一种新的替代算法（Helmholtz-curl 混合公式系统）：空气域电磁场由**低频也适定的 Helmholtz 方程**控制，大地域由适定的 curl-curl 方程控制。由于充分考虑了空气与大地物理性质的差异，混合系统数值稳定：条件数几乎与空气电导率无关、显著小于 Curl 系统；Curl 系统低频不稳定、正确性严重依赖空气电导率取值。陆地地形、海底地形、矿化模型的应用表明它是二维 MT 正演求解器的替代算法。传统上，curl-curl 方程在空气低频极限的稳定性靠拉格朗日乘子显式强加无散条件改善；本方案的改进来自空气中适定的 Helmholtz 方程。由于克服了低频空气域失稳问题，混合系统有望解决**三维 MT 正演中众所周知的数值失稳难题**。三维推导不如二维方便（无 TE/TM 解耦），但流程几乎相同：(1) 空气选稳定矢量 Helmholtz 方程、大地选稳定 curl-curl 方程；(2) 混合有限元离散、特别注意空气-大地界面的连续性条件。向三维地电磁问题的推广将在未来工作中讨论。
