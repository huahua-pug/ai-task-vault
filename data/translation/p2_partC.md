# 英中对照·分片3：第4章（腔体问题与柯西数据）、第5章（结果）、第6章（结论）

## 4. The cavity problem｜第4章 腔体问题

[EN] Once J_s and M_s are computed, the inverse problem reduces to the cavity problem (8)-(9). From the vector wave equation and its boundary condition one obtains the variational functional ⟨公式(18)：F(E) = ½∫_V[(∇×E)·(∇×E) − k_b²εr E·E]dV − jk0η0∫_S J·E dS⟩, and the reconstructed tangential electric field on S: ⟨公式(19)：E^recons_t = −M_s + n×E^inc⟩.

[中] 求得 J_s、M_s 后，逆问题化为腔体问题(8)-(9)。由矢量波动方程与边界条件可直接写出变分泛函 ⟨公式(18)⟩，以及 S 上重建出的切向总电场 ⟨公式(19)⟩。

[EN] 4.1 Finite element spaces: V is discretized by tetrahedra. The functional is bounded if E and ∇×E are square integrable, i.e., E ∈ H(curl, V); curl-conforming lowest-order edge elements N(r) (ensuring tangential continuity) discretize E ⟨公式(20)⟩; edge elements relate to RWG functions on S by ⟨公式(21)：β = n×N⟩. The permittivity is expanded with pulse functions per tetrahedron ⟨公式(22)⟩. Discrete inner products in L²(V) and L²_t(S) with adjoint relation ⟨公式(23)-(28)⟩ (D = diag of element volumes; S_ij surface mass matrix).

[中] 4.1 有限元空间：V 用四面体剖分。E 与 ∇×E 平方可积（即 E ∈ H(curl,V)）时泛函有界；用保证切向连续的最低阶旋度协调棱边元离散 E ⟨公式(20)⟩；棱边元与面上 RWG 函数的关系为 ⟨公式(21)：β = n×N⟩。介电常数按四面体用脉冲基展开 ⟨公式(22)⟩。给出 L²(V) 与 L²_t(S) 的离散内积、范数与伴随关系 ⟨公式(23)-(28)⟩（D 为单元体积对角阵，S_ij 为面质量矩阵）。

[EN] 4.2 The Gauss–Newton method: Rayleigh–Ritz on (18) yields the nonlinear system ⟨公式(29)：[R + K(εr)] E_sim = f⟩ with R the curl-curl stiffness matrix, K the permittivity-dependent mass-type matrix and f the surface current excitation. Cost functional ⟨公式(33)：C(εr) = Σ_s ‖d_s(εr)‖²_S = Σ_s ‖E^recons_{t,s} − P E_{sim,s}‖²_S⟩, where d_s is the discrepancy between the boundary-integral reconstructed surface field and the FEM-simulated one, and P projects volume fields onto tangential fields on S. First-order Taylor expansion linearizes the cost around εr⁽ⁿ⁾ ⟨公式(34)⟩; the Fréchet derivative ⟨公式(35)：K^E_{sip} = −∫_{V_p} k_b² N_i (Σ_j E_{sj}N_j) dV_p⟩ maps material properties to the surface field; its discrete adjoint ⟨公式(36)：F*_s = D⁻¹ F^H_s S⟩. Minimizing (34) is ill-conditioned (projection P and P^H); a GMRES Krylov method regularizes, the Krylov dimension l acting as regularization parameter chosen by the discrepancy principle using the estimated noise level δ ⟨公式(37)：residual ≤ τδ⟩. Update ⟨公式(38)：εr⁽ⁿ⁺¹⁾ = εr⁽ⁿ⁾ + α Δεr⟩ with a line search based on a second-order Taylor expansion of C(δα) ⟨公式(39)-(42)⟩; line search is used sparingly since each step requires an inverse solution of (29). [R+K(εr⁽ⁿ⁾)] is sparse symmetric — LU via Intel MKL PARDISO; K is reassembled each Gauss–Newton step from stored 6×6×T element contributions.

[中] 4.2 Gauss–Newton 法：对泛函(18)用 Rayleigh–Ritz 技巧得到非线性方程组 ⟨公式(29)⟩（R 为旋度-旋度刚度阵，K 为含介电常数的质量型矩阵，f 为面电流激励）。定义代价泛函 ⟨公式(33)⟩：d_s 是"边界积分重建的面场"与"FEM 模拟面场"之差，P 把体内场投影到 S 的切向场。在 εr⁽ⁿ⁾ 处一阶泰勒展开线性化 ⟨公式(34)⟩；Fréchet 导数 ⟨公式(35)⟩ 把材料属性映射到面电场；其离散伴随 ⟨公式(36)⟩。由于投影矩阵 P 与 P^H 的存在，极小化 (34) 病态——用 GMRES Krylov 方法正则化，Krylov 维数 l 即正则化参数，由偏差原理按估计噪声 δ 确定终止准则 ⟨公式(37)⟩。更新式 ⟨公式(38)⟩ 配线搜索（对 C(δα) 二阶泰勒求最优步长 ⟨公式(39)-(42)⟩）；线搜索每步需重解 (29)，故尽量少用。[R+K] 稀疏对称，用 Intel MKL PARDISO 做 LU 分解；K 在每次 GN 迭代前由预存的 6×6×T 单元贡献数组重装。

[EN] 4.3 Exploiting Cauchy data: (i) Noise level — since V is source-free for total fields, the Lorentz reciprocity functional ⟨公式(43)：R(Ei,Hj) = ∫_S (Ei×Hj − Ej×Hi)dS⟩ should vanish; with reconstruction errors it deviates. The reciprocity matrix L_ij = R(Ei,Hj) gives the noise estimate ⟨公式(44)：δ ≈ ΣΣ|L_ij| / (Ts(Ts−1))⟩ over all Ts incident fields. (ii) Initial guess — reciprocity between total and incident fields with a Born-type approximation and constant εr⁽⁰⁾ gives ⟨公式(45)-(46)：εr⁽⁰⁾ = 1 + jωμ0 Σ_i R(Ei,H^inc_i) / ∫_V k_b² E^inc_i·E^inc_i dV⟩. (iii) Lossy determination — for homogeneous lossless background, the time-average complex power flow of the incident field vanishes ⟨公式(47)：P^in + P^out = 0⟩; with the scatterer present ⟨公式(48)⟩ P^out is complex: its real part is radiated power plus material losses. If |P^out_i| < |P^in_i| the material is lossy; if |P^out| and |P^in| are very close for more than 75% of all excitations, the object is considered lossless and the permittivity forced purely real.

[中] 4.3 柯西数据的利用：(i) **噪声水平**——V 对总场无源，洛伦兹互易泛函 ⟨公式(43)⟩ 应为零；重建的面电流带误差，故其偏离零。构造互易矩阵 L_ij = R(Ei,Hj)，噪声估计为 ⟨公式(44)⟩（对全部 Ts 个入射场求平均）。(ii) **初值**——总场与入射场的互易关系加 Born 型近似、假设 εr⁽⁰⁾ 为常数，得 ⟨公式(45)(46)⟩ 的解析初值公式。(iii) **有耗判定**——均匀无耗背景下入射场的时间平均复功率流满足 ⟨公式(47)：P^in + P^out = 0⟩；物体存在时 ⟨公式(48)⟩ 的 P^out 为复数，实部代表流出功率与材料损耗。若 |P^out_i| < |P^in_i| 则材料有耗；若在超过 75% 的激励下 |P^out| 与 |P^in| 十分接近，则判定无耗、强制介电常数为纯实数。

## 5. Results｜第5章 结果

[EN] Multi-frequency data are used via the frequency-hopping technique ⟨公式(49)：εr = Re{εr} + j (f_k/f_{k+1}) Im{εr}⟩ from the lowest to the highest frequency, each result seeding the next. Physical constraints: Re{εr·ε_b} ≥ 0, Im{εr·ε_b} ≤ 0, projected each iteration. Limits: 32 Gauss–Newton iterations, 4 line-search steps; termination when C_n ⟨公式(50)⟩ < 10⁻³ or |Δεr|/|εr⁽ⁿ⁾| < 10⁻⁵. Platform: one core 3.30 GHz, 8 GB RAM, C++; synthetic data from an FEBI solver with additive complex Gaussian noise ⟨公式(51)⟩; model error ⟨公式(52)：err = ‖εr^{rec} − εr^{true}‖/‖εr^{true}‖⟩.

[中] 多频数据用**频率跳跃**技术 ⟨公式(49)⟩ 从低频到高频依次反演，上一频点结果作为下一频点初值（虚部按频率比缩放）。物理约束：Re{εr·ε_b} ≥ 0、Im{εr·ε_b} ≤ 0，每次迭代强制投影。迭代上限：GN 32 次、线搜索 4 次；终止条件：归一化代价 ⟨公式(50)⟩ < 10⁻³ 或更新量相对值 < 10⁻⁵。平台：单核 3.30 GHz、8 GB 内存、C++；合成数据由 FEBI 求解器生成并加复高斯噪声 ⟨公式(51)⟩；模型误差定义 ⟨公式(52)⟩。

[EN] Synthetic examples: (1) Concealed target — a lossy cube (εr = 2.0 − j0.8, 30 mm) inside a lossless cube (εr = 1.5, 100 mm), free-space background, 5 GHz, 36 plane waves, 648 measurement points on a 1 m sphere, noise ξ = 0.10; unknowns Ns = 9540 (surface), Ne = 20609 (field), Nv = 19667 (material); model error 15.0%, with the reciprocity initial guess εr⁽⁰⁾ = 1.593 − j0.594 (between the two true values). With true surface currents the error drops to 9.4% — the first step introduces some model error. Noise study: the error stays nearly flat up to ξ ≈ 0.7, i.e., the decomposition also robustifies against strong noise. (2) Inhomogeneous background (inner εb = 1.25): error 15.8%. (3) Eight prisms (εr = 2.0 − j0.75, 18 mm gaps): with the reconstruction surface close to the prisms, missing reactive currents (evanescent) degrade the result to 29.7%; moving the current-reconstruction surface ~1.5 wavelengths away (280 mm FEM domain, only the inner 100 mm reconstructed; Ns = 74160, Ne = 579442, Nv = 21514) recovers 16.5% — at the cost of ts = 1507 s and tv = 22636 s. (4) L-shaped material (εr = 1.8 − j0.67), two frequencies 5 and 6 GHz — 6 GHz being an interior resonance of the reconstruction domain: EFIE-only (ignoring the second row of P^c) produces spurious solutions, while the combined EFIE+MFIE works; model error 11%. Table 1 summarizes all cases.

[中] 合成算例：(1) **隐藏目标**——有耗小立方（εr = 2.0 − j0.8，30 mm）藏于无耗大立方（εr = 1.5，100 mm），自由空间背景，5 GHz，36 个平面波，1 m 球面上 648 个测量点，噪声 ξ = 0.10；未知量 Ns = 9540 / Ne = 20609 / Nv = 19667；模型误差 15.0%，互易初值 εr⁽⁰⁾ = 1.593 − j0.594（恰在两真值之间）。用真面电流时误差降到 9.4%——第一步引入了模型误差。噪声研究：误差在 ξ ≈ 0.7 之前几乎不涨，即分解法同时对强噪声鲁棒。(2) **非均匀背景**（内背景 εb = 1.25）：误差 15.8%。(3) **八棱柱**（εr = 2.0 − j0.75，间距 18 mm）：重建面贴近棱柱时，缺失的反应性电流（倏逝）使误差达 29.7%；把电流重建面挪远约 1.5 个波长（280 mm FEM 域、只重建内部 100 mm；Ns = 74160 / Ne = 579442 / Nv = 21514）后恢复到 16.5%——代价是 ts = 1507 s、tv = 22636 s。(4) **L 形材料**（εr = 1.8 − j0.67），双频 5 与 6 GHz——6 GHz 恰为重建域内谐振频率：仅用 EFIE（忽略 P^c 第二行）出现伪解，EFIE+MFIE 联合则正常；模型误差 11%。表 1 汇总全部算例。

[EN] Experimental data (Fresnel Institute): TwoCubes — two lossless cubes (25 mm, εr = 2.3); 81 illumination directions, 36 measurement points on a circle (co- and cross-polarized sets merged via reciprocity), 13 frequencies from 5 to 8 GHz; FEM domain: 140 mm sphere, reconstruction domain: 80 mm cube; Ns = 7752, Ne = 154183, Nv = 78594. The energy-conservation test determined the material to be lossless, so the imaginary part was forced to zero; locations, shapes and permittivity values were reconstructed approximately. IsocaSphere — twelve spheres (23.8 mm, εr = 2.6) at icosahedron vertices; similar setup. Compared to other techniques on the Fresnel dataset (e.g., [19]), the proposed method is currently slightly less successful; equipping the Krylov regularization with multiplicative (weighted L2 norm) regularization is expected to further improve performance.

[中] 实验数据（Fresnel 研究所基准）：**TwoCubes**——两个无耗立方（25 mm，εr = 2.3）；81 个照射方向、圆周上 36 个测量点（同极化与交叉极化数据经互易关系合并）、5–8 GHz 共 13 个频点；FEM 域为 140 mm 球、重建域为 80 mm 立方；Ns = 7752 / Ne = 154183 / Nv = 78594。能量守恒检验判定材料无耗，故强制虚部为零；散射体位置、形状与介电常数均得到近似重建。**IsocaSphere**——十二个球（23.8 mm，εr = 2.6）置于二十面体顶点，设置类似。与 Fresnel 数据集上其他方法（如 [19]）相比，本方法目前重建效果略逊；为 Krylov 正则化配备乘性（加权 L2 范数）正则化有望进一步提升。

## 6. Conclusions｜第6章 结论

[EN] An inverse scattering technique exploiting the equivalence principle was investigated, based on the boundary integral method and the finite element method, retaining the advantages of both while eliminating their drawbacks. Love's equivalence principle eliminated non-radiating sources; the MLFMM decreased the computational burden of the integral equation; the second part was converted into a variational form and solved by the FEM without any artificial boundary condition, iteratively via the Gauss–Newton method. Promising results on synthetic and experimental data. If evanescent fields are present on the decoupling surface, the reconstruction quality can be adversely influenced — overcome by shifting the decoupling surface slightly away from the objects. Future work: edge-preserving regularization (e.g., weighted L2 norm); an efficient material representation independent of the number of tetrahedral elements (Fourier series or wavelet expansion), since the λ/10 mesh density required for the field solution over-parameterizes the material expansion.

[中] 本文研究了利用等效原理的逆散射技术，基于边界积分法与有限元法，兼取两者之长、互补其短。Love 等效原理消除了非辐射源；MLFMM 降低了积分方程的计算负担；第二部分化为变分形式，用 FEM 求解（无需任何人工边界条件），并以 Gauss–Newton 法迭代。合成与实验数据均获得有希望的结果。若解耦面上存在倏逝场，重建质量会受影响——把解耦面稍远离物体即可克服。未来工作：①边缘保持正则化（如加权 L2 范数）；②与四面体数目无关的高效材料参数表示（Fourier 级数或小波展开）——因为场解所需的 λ/10 网格密度使材料展开严重过参数化。
