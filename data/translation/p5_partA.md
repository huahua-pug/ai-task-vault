# 求解多流体等离子体模型的连续-间断混合有限元方法（英中对照·分片1：摘要、引言、模型与方法A）

体例：[EN] 原文（公式用字体安全 Unicode），[中] 汉译。

## Abstract｜摘要

[EN] The multi-fluid plasma model represents electrons, multiple ion species, and multiple neutral species as separate fluids interacting through short-range collisions and long-range electromagnetic fields. The model spans a large range of temporal and spatial scales, which renders it stiff. To address the timescales, a blended continuous and discontinuous Galerkin method is proposed: massive ion and neutral species are modeled with an explicit discontinuous Galerkin (DG) method while the electrons and electromagnetic fields are modeled with an implicit continuous Galerkin (CG) method. This captures large-gradient ion/neutral physics (shock formation) while resolving high-frequency electron dynamics efficiently. The Blended Finite Element Method (BFEM) is benchmarked for accuracy on the two-fluid 1D soliton problem and the electromagnetic shock tube problem, compared to conventional FV and FE methods; it is particularly effective for stiff problems with realistic physical parameters (realistic electron mass and speed of light). A three-fluid application demonstrates species separation in multi-component plasmas.

[中] 多流体等离子体模型把电子、多种离子与多种中性粒子表示为各自独立的流体，它们通过短程碰撞与长程电磁场相互作用。模型跨越极大的时空尺度，因而具有刚性。为应对时间尺度问题，本文提出连续-间断混合有限元方法（BFEM）：质量大的离子与中性流体用显式间断伽辽金（DG）方法模拟，电子流体与电磁场用隐式连续伽辽金（CG）方法模拟。该方案既能捕捉离子/中性流体的大梯度物理（激波形成），又能高效分辨高频电子动力学。文中给出 BFEM 细节；以双流体一维孤子问题与电磁激波管问题做精度基准，并与常规有限体积、纯 DG 方法比较——BFEM 在采用真实物理参数（真实电子质量与光速）的刚性问题中特别有效。三流体应用演示了多组分等离子体中的组分分离。

## 1. Introduction｜引言

[EN] Plasma model hierarchy: kinetic (Boltzmann–Maxwell, 6D phase space, expensive) → two-fluid (first three velocity moments, local thermodynamic equilibrium per species) → MHD (neglect electron inertia, infinite speed of light → generalized Ohm's law, displacement current dropped, charge neutrality enforced; ideal MHD limited to high collisionality, small Larmor radius, low resistivity) → multi-fluid (multiple ions/neutrals evolved separately, interacting via collisions and fields; no asymptotic assumptions on light speed — captures waves faster than the magnetosonic speed). Characteristic speeds: species acoustic speeds and light speed; characteristic frequencies: plasma and cyclotron — all severely limit the time step. PDE type: inhomogeneous hyperbolic balance laws; methods: FV (wave propagation; source splitting → phase errors at high frequency), CG (smooth solutions, high order, but no dissipation → artificial dissipation needed; global solve couples naturally with implicit integration, no CFL restriction), DG (shock capturing + conservation + high order; explicit CFL ≤ 1/(2p−1); implicit DG difficult: limiters → ill-conditioned Jacobian, Newton may not converge [35]).

[中] 等离子体模型层级：动理学（Boltzmann–Maxwell，六维相空间，代价高）→ 双流体（取前三阶速度矩，各组分内部局部热平衡）→ MHD（忽略电子惯性、光速无穷 → 广义欧姆定律、位移电流消失、强制电中性；理想 MHD 只适用于高碰撞率、小拉莫尔半径、低电阻率区间）→ 多流体（多种离子/中性各自演化、经碰撞与场耦合；对光速不做渐近假设——能捕捉快于磁声速的波）。特征速度：各组分流速、声速与光速；特征频率：等离子体频率与回旋频率——全都严重限制时间步长。PDE 类型：非均匀双曲平衡律系统。方法对比：有限体积（波传播法；源项需分裂→高频相位误差）；CG（光滑解、高阶、无耗散→需人工耗散抑制振荡；全局求解天然配隐式时间积分、无 CFL 限制）；DG（激波捕捉+守恒+高阶；显式 CFL ≤ 1/(2p−1)；隐式 DG 困难：限制器使雅可比病态、Newton 可能不收敛 [35]）。

[EN] An ideal method for the multi-fluid model: high-order, shock capturing, good resolution of fast oscillations, no restrictive time step. The BFEM: electron fluid + EM fields (fast, smooth — no shocks) modeled by implicit CG; ions and neutrals (slow, shock-forming) by explicit DG. IMEX precedent: advection–diffusion (implicit diffusion, explicit advection), geometry-induced stiffness. In many configurations (Z-pinches, tokamaks, stellarators, ICF capsules) electrons and fields have no sharp gradients — CG suitable, no limiters, well-conditioned Newton Jacobian. The BFEM removes the strictest time-step limitations (light speed, electron acoustic speed, electron plasma and cyclotron frequencies). The paper: Section 2 model; Section 3 BFEM implementation (CG/DG portions and coupling); Section 4 benchmarks (two-fluid soliton, EM shock tube) and a three-fluid species separation application [42]; Section 5 conclusions.

[中] 多流体模型的"理想数值方法"应具备：高阶精度、能捕捉激波、能分辨快振荡、无苛刻时间步限制。为此提出 BFEM（一维）：**电子流体与电磁场用隐式 CG，全部离子与中性流体用显式 DG**——这一混合离散由电子、离子、中性与场的物理性质决定。IMEX 先例：对流-扩散问题（隐式扩散项+显式对流项）、几何诱导刚性。在诸多关注构型（Z 箍缩、托卡马克、仿星器、惯性约束聚变靶丸）中，电子流体与电磁场没有尖锐梯度——适合 CG，无需限制器，Newton 雅可比良态。BFEM 移除了最苛刻的时间步限制（光速、电子声速、电子等离子体与回旋频率）。章节安排：第 2 章模型；第 3 章 BFEM 实现（CG/DG 两部分及耦合）；第 4 章基准测试（双流体孤子、电磁激波管）与三流体组分分离应用 [42]；第 5 章结论。

## 2. Multi-fluid plasma model｜多流体等离子体模型

[EN] Each species α evolves mass, momentum and energy via the first three velocity moments of the Vlasov equation ⟨公式(1)-(5)：∂ρ/∂t + ∇·(ρu) = 0；∂ρu/∂t + ∇·(ρuu + pI) = (ρq/m)(E + u×B)；∂ε/∂t + ∇·((ε+p)u) = (ρq/m)u·E；ε = p/(Γ−1) + ½ρu²⟩. Maxwell equations ⟨公式(6)-(9)⟩ are overdetermined (6 unknowns, 8 equations): divergence constraints are analytically preserved but round-off violates them — cast in purely hyperbolic form ⟨公式(10)-(13)：∂B/∂t + ∇×E + γ∇Ψ = 0；(1/c²)∂E/∂t − ∇×B + χ∇Φ = −μ0Σ(ραqα/mα)uα；(1/χ)∂Φ/∂t + ∇·E = Σρq/m；(1/γc²)∂Ψ/∂t + ∇·B = 0⟩ with error-correction potentials Φ, Ψ and dimensionless speeds χ, γ > 1 (field divergence errors propagate at ±χc, ±γc). Balance-law form ⟨公式(14)：∂Q/∂t + ∇·F(Q) = S(Q)⟩; homogeneous part hyperbolic; source depends only on Q. Flux Jacobian eigenvalues: combinations of flow/acoustic/light/divergence speeds. Source Jacobian eigenvalues purely imaginary — first three 0, ±iω_p ⟨公式(15)⟩ — dispersion is physical (plasma waves), not a numerical artifact. Timescales span extremely fast EM waves and electron response to much slower ions/neutrals: the model is mathematically stiff — explicit methods must resolve the shortest timescale, implicit methods must invert a poorly conditioned matrix.

[中] 各组分 α 的质量、动量、能量演化由 Vlasov 方程前三阶速度矩给出 ⟨公式(1)-(5)⟩。Maxwell 方程 ⟨公式(6)-(9)⟩ 超定（6 个未知量 8 条方程）：散度约束解析上自动保持，但舍入误差会破坏之——改写为**纯双曲形式** ⟨公式(10)-(13)⟩，引入误差修正势 Φ、Ψ 把散度误差传出计算域，无量纲参数 χ、γ > 1 决定误差传播速度 ±χc、±γc。平衡律形式 ⟨公式(14)⟩；齐次部分双曲；源项只依赖 Q、可局部计算。通量雅可比特征值是流速/声速/光速/散度速度的组合；**源雅可比特征值全为纯虚数**——前三个为 0、±iω_p ⟨公式(15)⟩，说明色散是物理行为（等离子体波）而非数值伪影。特征时间从极快的电磁波与电子响应到慢得多的离子/中性响应：模型数学上**刚性**——显式方法必须分辨最短时间尺度，隐式方法必须求逆病态矩阵。
