## 2.2 GMRES solution｜GMRES 求解与预条件

[EN] System (22) is solved by restarted GMRES with block-diagonal preconditioning M⁻¹Ax = M⁻¹b ⟨公式(25)-(26)⟩; the Gram matrices are very well conditioned. Writing A = M − N ⟨公式(27)⟩, λ(M⁻¹A) = 1 − λ(M⁻¹N) ⟨公式(28)⟩ and ⟨公式(29)：∀n ≥ 1, Re(a_n) = Re(a) > 0 ⟹ ρ(M⁻¹N) < 1⟩ (proven in Appendix A), hence the eigenvalues of M⁻¹A lie in the disk centered at 1 with radius ρ(M⁻¹N) ⟨公式(30)⟩ — this also proves convergence of the Jacobi/GS iteration. Per GMRES iteration, the preconditioning requires solving G_n y = x, G_f y = x, and A_n y = x — the latter by CG preconditioned by the diagonal (warm start y⁰ = y^{ℓ−1}). Memory ⟨公式(32)⟩ ≃ N_inc·restart + max[N_n/2, N(S_f)N(S_{N−1})] (the dense C₁, C₂ computed and stored out-of-core); an inexact CG (ϵ_CG = 0.1) reduces time. The curl2div/div2curl round-trip error err^T_n ⟨公式(33)⟩ is monitored.

[中] 用重启 GMRES 解 (22)，块对角预条件 ⟨公式(25)(26)⟩；Gram 矩阵非常良态。写 A = M − N ⟨公式(27)⟩，则 λ(M⁻¹A) = 1 − λ(M⁻¹N) ⟨公式(28)⟩，且**附录 A 证明 ⟨公式(29)：Re(a_n) > 0 ⟹ 谱半径 ρ(M⁻¹N) < 1⟩**——于是预条件系统特征值落在以 1 为心的单位圆盘内 ⟨公式(30)⟩（该结论同时证明 Jacobi/GS 迭代的收敛性）。每次 GMRES 迭代的预条件只需解 G_n、G_f（Gram 矩阵）与 A_n 子系统（对角预条件 CG，热启动 y⁰ = y^{ℓ−1}）。内存 ⟨公式(32)⟩ ≃ N_inc·restart + max[N_n/2, N(S_f)N(S_{N−1})]（稠密矩阵 C₁、C₂ 离线计算存储）；CG 用不精确求解（ϵ_CG = 0.1）降低耗时。curl2div 与 div2curl 往返误差 err^T_n ⟨公式(33)⟩ 被量化监控。

## 3. Analytical planar case｜平面解析情形：收敛率与系数优化

[EN] For the infinite planar coating (translationally invariant), modes decouple: solutions e^{ixk_x}(Ae^{izk_z} + Be^{−izk_z}), s = k_x/k0; s ∈ [0,1] propagating, s > 1 evanescent ⟨公式(34)(35)⟩. LIBC and TCs give relations ⟨公式(36)-(38)⟩; the IR condition on Sf ⟨公式(39)(40)⟩; the per-mode system ⟨公式(41)⟩ yields eigenvalues ⟨公式(42)-(43)：λ(s) = 1 − ζ(s)⟩. For strongly evanescent modes (s ≫ max|√(ε_iμ_i)|), the mode sees only two semi-infinite media, giving ⟨公式(44)(45)：λ(s) ~ λ∞(s) = −g_p/s⟩ with g_TM = iRe(a₁)ε_m, g_TE = iRe(a₁)μ_m/|a₁|² ⟨公式(46)：ε_m, μ_m = signed averages of the two adjacent media⟩. Since all a_n equal implies λ_max < 2 ⟨公式(50)⟩ and λ_min ~ γ/s_M ⟨公式(49)⟩, the GMRES convergence-rate estimate κ(s_M) ⟨公式(51)⟩ behaves as ⟨公式(52)：κ ~ 1 − 2√(γ/(s_M λ_max))⟩ — i.e., ⟨公式(53)：Log(r_global) ~ −2ℓ√(γ/(s_M λ_max))⟩: **arithmetically slow convergence** dominated by evanescent modes (propagating modes converge fast initially, Appendix B).

[中] 无限平面涂层沿 x、y 平移不变，模式解耦：解形如 e^{ixk_x}(Ae^{izk_z} + Be^{−izk_z})，s = k_x/k0；s ∈ [0,1] 为传播模，s > 1 为倏逝模 ⟨公式(34)(35)⟩。LIBC 与传输条件给出关系 ⟨公式(36)-(38)⟩，Sf 上的 IR 条件 ⟨公式(39)(40)⟩，逐模系统 ⟨公式(41)⟩ 的特征值 ⟨公式(42)(43)⟩。对强倏逝模（s ≫ max|√(ε_iμ_i)|），波"看不到"内外边界、只看到两个半无限介质，得 ⟨公式(44)(45)：λ(s) ~ −g_p/s⟩，其中 g_TM = iRe(a₁)ε_m、g_TE = iRe(a₁)μ_m/|a₁|² ⟨公式(46)——ε_m、μ_m 是相邻两介质带符号平均⟩。当所有 a_n 相同时 λ_max < 2 ⟨公式(50)⟩，而 λ_min ~ γ/s_M ⟨公式(49)⟩，于是 GMRES 收敛率估计 κ(s_M) ⟨公式(51)⟩ 满足 ⟨公式(52)⟩，即残差 ⟨公式(53)：Log(r) ~ −2ℓ√(γ/(s_M λ_max))⟩——**按算术速度缓慢收敛，瓶颈在倏逝模**（传播模初期收敛快，见附录 B）。

[EN] Optimized coefficients: minimizing κ amounts to maximizing γ; setting |g_TM| ≃ |g_TE| with a₁ real positive gives ⟨公式(54)：a¹_opt ≃ a¹_∞ = √(|μ_m/ε_m|)⟩ (for a free-space interface a∞ = 1). More accurate values a_opt_n(s_M) minimize κ(s_M) over the coefficient set; s∞ is the threshold beyond which a_opt_n(s_M) is constant ⟨公式(55)⟩. For arbitrary objects, the tangent plane approximation (TPA) applies the planar results locally; its validity is checked on a coated sphere against a Mie-series modal analysis ⟨公式(56)-(61)：s ≃ n/(k0R0)⟩ — Table 3 shows ρ(M⁻¹N) of plane and sphere agree even at k0R0 = 0.52. A heuristic for the mesh-truncated s_M ≤ s_h = λ0/h ⟨公式(62)⟩: compute g_iter(s_M) = n_iter(s_M)/n_iter(a=1) on a small "twin" object A with the same coating, pick s^opt_M minimizing it ⟨公式(64)(65)⟩; the empirical a_ref = √((ε₁+ε₂)(μ₁+μ₂)/2) ⟨公式(63)⟩ always yields more iterations than a_opt.

[中] **优化系数**：极小化 κ 等价于极大化 γ；令 |g_TM| ≃ |g_TE| 且 a₁ 取正实数，得 ⟨公式(54)：a¹_opt ≃ a¹_∞ = √(|μ_m/ε_m|)⟩（自由空间界面时 a∞ = 1）。更精细的 a_opt_n(s_M) 通过对系数组极小化 κ(s_M) 得到；s∞ 是 a_opt_n(s_M) 趋于常数的阈值 ⟨公式(55)⟩。对任意物体，用**切平面近似（TPA）**局部套用平面结果；在涂覆球上与 Mie 级数模态分析 ⟨公式(56)-(61)：s ≃ n/(k0R0)⟩ 对照验证 TPA 的有效性——表 3 显示平面与球的 ρ(M⁻¹N) 即使在 k0R0 = 0.52 时也一致。结合网格截断 s_M ≤ s_h = λ0/h ⟨公式(62)⟩，给出启发式流程：在与大目标同涂层的"小孪生"物体上扫描 g_iter(s_M) = n_iter(s_M)/n_iter(a=1) ⟨公式(64)(65)⟩，取最小者对应的 a_opt；经验公式 a_ref ⟨公式(63)⟩ 的迭代数总是最多。

## 4. Numerical results｜数值结果

[EN] Objects: 25 cm sphere, cone-sphere, stopper (Fig. 4), PEC cores with coatings C1/C2 of constant materials (M1: ε=1−i, μ=(1−i)/2; M3: ε=2, μ=1/2; M6: ε=μ=2; M7, M9 lossy); restart = 10; inexact CG (ϵ_CG = 0.1, erK = 10⁻³). Accuracy: bistatic RCS vs Mie series (sphere) and BOR-FEIE (axisymmetric) agree well ⟨Figs. 6-8⟩; the curl2div round-trip error err^T_n decreases with mesh refinement and grows at singular interfaces (Table 2). Coefficient selection: g_iter(s_M) behaves similarly for geometrically different objects with the same coating; a∞ from (54) is a good approximation of the limit; iterations increase with λ0/h (since s_M = O(λ0/h) and κ = 1 − o(s^{−1/2})); g_iter(s^opt_M) decreases with frequency. Final RCS computations use the a_opt chosen on the small twin object. Convergence curves confirm the arithmetic-rate theory ⟨Fig. 13⟩.

[中] 算例：25 cm 球、锥-球、stopper（图 4），PEC 核 + 常数材料涂层 C1/C2（M1: ε=1−i, μ=(1−i)/2；M3: ε=2, μ=1/2；M6: ε=μ=2；M7、M9 有耗）；restart = 10；不精确 CG（ϵ_CG = 0.1, erK = 10⁻³）。**精度**：双站 RCS 与 Mie 级数（球）及 BOR-FEIE（轴对称体）吻合良好（图 6-8）；curl2div 往返误差 err^T_n 随网格加密下降、界面有奇异点时增大（表 2）。**系数选取**：同涂层、几何迥异的物体上 g_iter(s_M) 行为相似；由 (54) 得到的 a∞ 是极限值的良好近似；迭代数随 λ0/h 增大（因 s_M = O(λ0/h) 且 κ = 1 − o(s^{−1/2})）；g_iter(s^opt_M) 随频率升高而下降。最终 RCS 计算采用在小孪生体上选出的 a_opt。收敛曲线证实算术收敛率理论（图 13）。

## 5. Conclusions｜第5章 结论

[EN] With the same memory requirements, the GMRES preconditioned by the block-diagonal of the global matrix yields a much faster solution than the Gauss–Seidel algorithm. The accuracy on the RCS shows the curl2div transformation is efficient. The convergence rate depends on the TC coefficients a_i; via the tangent plane approximation, a procedure computes optimal values allowing, for a reasonable cost, significant acceleration when the mesh is dense. Setting a_i = a∞_i always yields faster convergence than a_i = 1 or a_i = a_ref. Future work: parallelization of the code; reducing the IR matrix-vector complexity by FMM or ACA; and making the iteration count mesh-independent by implementing non-local transmission conditions (yet to be defined), similar to those proposed for the Helmholtz problem.

[中] 在相同内存开销下，块对角预条件的 GMRES 比 Gauss–Seidel 快得多。RCS 精度证明 curl2div 变换有效。收敛率依赖 TC 系数 a_i；借助切平面近似提出了优化系数的计算流程，在网格致密时以合理代价显著加速。取 a_i = a∞_i 总是快于 a_i = 1 或 a_i = a_ref。未来工作：代码并行化；用 FMM 或 ACA 降低 IR 矩阵-向量积复杂度；实现"非局部传输条件"使迭代数与网格无关（类似 Helmholtz 问题中已有提案，此处尚待定义）。
