# 结合逆等效电流与有限元方法求解三维逆散射问题（英中对照·分片1：摘要与引言）

体例：[EN] 原文（公式用字体安全 Unicode 写出：∇× 旋度、∇· 散度、∂/∂t 时间导数、∫ 面积分/体积分），[中] 汉译；术语首现括注英文。

## Abstract｜摘要

[EN] An approach combining boundary integral and finite element methods is introduced for the solution of three-dimensional inverse electromagnetic medium scattering problems. Based on the equivalence principle, unknown equivalent electric and magnetic surface current densities on a closed surface are utilized to decompose the inverse medium problem into two parts: a linear radiation problem and a nonlinear cavity problem. The first problem is formulated by a boundary integral equation, the computational burden of which is reduced by employing the multilevel fast multipole method (MLFMM). Reconstructed Cauchy data on the surface allows the utilization of the Lorentz reciprocity and the Poynting's theorems. Exploiting these theorems, the noise level and an initial guess are estimated for the cavity problem. Moreover, it is possible to determine whether the material is lossy or not. In the second problem, the estimated surface currents form inhomogeneous boundary conditions of the cavity problem. The cavity problem is formulated by the finite element technique and solved iteratively by the Gauss–Newton method to reconstruct the properties of the object. Regularization for both the first and the second problems is achieved by a Krylov subspace method. The proposed method is tested against both synthetic and experimental data and promising reconstruction results are obtained.

[中] 本文提出一种边界积分方程与有限元方法相结合的方案，用于求解三维逆电磁介质散射问题。基于等效原理，在封闭曲面上引入未知的等效电、磁面电流密度，把逆介质问题分解为两部分：一个线性辐射问题和一个非线性腔体问题。第一部分用边界积分方程表述，其计算负担通过多层快速多极子方法（MLFMM）降低。曲面上重建出的柯西数据（Cauchy data，即面上电磁场信息）使得洛伦兹互易定理与坡印廷定理得以应用：借此为腔体问题估计噪声水平与迭代初值，并且可以判定材料是否有耗。第二部分中，估计出的面电流构成腔体问题的非齐次边界条件。腔体问题用有限元技术表述，通过 Gauss–Newton 法迭代求解以重建物体属性。第一、第二部分的正则化均由 Krylov 子空间方法实现。方法在合成数据与实验数据上均得到验证，取得了有希望的重建结果。

## 1. Introduction｜引言

[EN] The determination of material properties of a domain of interest through the use of electromagnetic waves is called an inverse electromagnetic medium problem — an active research area due to applications in remote sensing, non-destructive testing, biomedicine, and through-wall imaging. Techniques can be classified into three main groups: (1) optimization methods, (2) decomposition methods, and (3) sampling methods. Optimization methods formulate the problem as a system of integral equations consisting of data and object equations solved by Newton-type methods, contrast source inversion techniques and other optimization techniques; they handle the ill-posedness and the nonlinearity of the inverse problem at the same time. Decomposition methods deal with them separately by dividing the problem into two parts. Since both require iterative approaches, sampling methods reconstruct geometrical properties of the scatterer by solving a series of ill-posed linear problems.

[中] 利用电磁波确定感兴趣区域内材料属性的问题称为**逆电磁介质问题**，因遥感、无损检测、生物医学、穿墙成像等应用而长期活跃。文献方法可分三类：(1) 优化方法；(2) 分解方法；(3) 采样方法。优化方法把问题表述为由数据方程与物体方程构成的积分方程组，用 Newton 型方法、对比源反演（contrast source inversion）等技术求解——不适定性与非线性被同时处理。分解方法则把问题一分为二，分别处理二者。由于前两类都需要迭代，采样方法通过求解一系列线性不适定问题重建散射体的几何属性。

[EN] The majority of optimization techniques are based on integral equations: the domain of interest can be kept to a minimum, but integral-equation formulations restrict the background media and are computationally expensive. Finite element and finite difference based inversion techniques have no such restriction, but the domain of interest must be larger. Various advances: FEM/FDM for medical imaging; Krylov regularization enhanced by multiplicative regularization; fast multipole for 2D imaging without inverting the forward operator; parallel Gauss–Newton with implicit Jacobian for 3D microwave imaging; discrete dipole approximation for clinical 3D imaging.

[中] 多数优化技术基于积分方程：感兴趣区域可以取到最小，但积分方程表述对背景介质有限制且计算昂贵。有限元/有限差分逆演无此限制，但计算域必须更大。相关进展包括：面向医学成像的 FEM/FDM 方法；用乘性正则化增强的 Krylov 子空间正则化；免于显式求逆的快速多极 2D 成像；带隐式雅可比计算的并行 Gauss–Newton 三维微波成像；用离散偶极近似加速的临床三维成像。

[EN] Inverse source problems exploit the equivalence principle for antenna diagnostics, near-field–far-field transformations and source reconstruction. Equivalence principle has also been considered within inverse medium scattering: an embedding technique with equivalent currents for 2D problems with inhomogeneous surroundings; partial volumetric-current reconstruction before material reconstruction; Born-type imaging of buried objects from Cauchy data; contrast source inversion with analytical continuation / single-layer potential to reconstruct Cauchy data — the main motivation there being to keep using the free-space Green's function even for inhomogeneous backgrounds. In this study the equivalence principle is invoked for a different main motivation: reducing the computational cost of 3D inverse scattering by separating the linear part from the nonlinear problem.

[中] 逆源问题利用等效原理做天线诊断、近远场变换与源重建；等效原理也被用于逆介质散射：面向复杂环境的等效电流嵌入技术、先部分重建体电流再重建材料属性的两步法、基于柯西数据的 Born 型埋地目标成像、以及用解析延拓/单层位势延拓柯西数据的对比源反演——后两者的动机是在非均匀背景下继续使用自由空间格林函数。需要强调：本文引用等效原理的主要动机不同——**通过把线性部分与非线性问题分离来降低三维逆散射的计算成本**（当然，具体引用方式与后两者有共同点，那里处理的是二维标量问题）。

[EN] The unknown object is illuminated by transmitters; scattered electric fields are measured in the far- or near-field region. A closed surface enclosing the object and excluding the sources is defined. Exploiting the equivalence principle, equivalent surface currents decompose the problem into: (1) a linear radiation problem outside the surface, solved by a boundary integral representation with Rao–Wilton–Glisson (RWG) basis functions and point testing; the MLFMM reduces storage/time from O(N²) to O(N log N). The radiation operator has a large null-space of non-radiating currents; forcing the fields of the surface currents to vanish inside the surface (Love's field equivalence principle) eliminates this null-space. Evanescent waves from reactive currents decay rapidly and cannot be reconstructed from measured data, making the inverse radiation problem ill-conditioned — GMRES regularizes it implicitly via its Krylov subspace.

[中] 未知物体被多个发射源照射，散射电场在远场或近场测量。定义一个包含物体、排除源的封闭曲面 S。利用等效原理，等效面电流把问题分解为：(1) S 外的**线性辐射问题**——用边界积分表述 + RWG 基函数 + 点测试离散；MLFMM 把存储与计算量从 O(N²) 降至 O(N log N)。辐射算子存在由非辐射电流构成的大零空间；强加"面电流在 S 内部产生零场"（Love 场等效原理）即可消除该零空间。反应性电流产生的倏逝波衰减极快、无法从测量数据重建，这使辐射逆问题病态——GMRES 通过其 Krylov 子空间实现隐式正则化。

[EN] After obtaining the surface currents, the inverse scattering problem reduces to a cavity problem with inhomogeneous boundary conditions. The proposed method has no constraint on the background medium in the domain of interest, uses a surface integral formulation instead of volume integrals, and sparse rather than dense matrices for the volume computations. The nonlinearity is handled in the cavity problem by the finite element formulation, which has lower computational burden and more flexibility for bounded domains than volumetric integral equations. Solving the exterior problem yields Cauchy data — both electric and magnetic currents on S — enabling relations based on the Lorentz reciprocity theorem and Poynting's theorem: the noise level (used to determine the regularization parameter) is estimated via the reciprocity relation for total fields of different excitations; a reciprocity relation with a Born-type approximation gives an initial guess; energy conservation via Poynting's formula determines whether the object is lossy. The first step can also serve as a pre-processing step for algorithms requiring both electric and magnetic fields on a closed surface.

[中] 求得面电流后，逆散射问题化为带非齐次边界条件的腔体问题。本方法对感兴趣区域的背景介质无限制；体积计算用面积分表述替代体积分、用稀疏矩阵替代稠密矩阵。逆问题的非线性由腔体问题中的有限元表述处理——对有界域，FEM 比体积分方程负担更低、更灵活。求解外问题得到柯西数据（S 上电、磁两种面流），据此可建立基于洛伦兹互易定理与坡印廷定理的多个关系式：用不同激励下总场的互易关系估计噪声水平（用于确定正则化参数）；用互易关系加 Born 型近似导出迭代初值；用坡印廷公式检验能量守恒以判断物体是否有耗。此外，第一步（由散射场重建柯西数据）可作为需要面上电、磁场的其他算法的预处理步骤。
