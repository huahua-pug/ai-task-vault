## 5. ChECSIM｜电荷与能量同时守恒的半隐式格式

[EN] The two reference schemes preserve either proper Gauss laws (CCLF) or the energy (ECSIM), but not both; ECSIM's Maxwell solver allows CFL-free steps, CCLF's does not. Goal: combine all advantages, keeping ECSIM's low cost (linear-implicit field iteration + explicit pusher). Obstacle: in ECSIM's discrete Ampère equation, charge-conserving deposition (44) is impossible without nonlinear resolution — the particle trajectory is unknown over the second half of the time-step. Remedy: a prediction/correction two-step scheme: guess step (X^n, V^n, e^n, b^n) ↦ (X^{n+1/2}, Ṽ^{n+1}, ẽ^{n+1}, b^{n+1}), then correction ↦ (X^{n+1}, V^{n+1}, e^{n+1}, b^{n+1}).

[中] 两个参考格式各保其一（CCLF 保 Gauss、ECSIM 保能量），且只有 ECSIM 的场求解器允许无 CFL 步长。目标：兼得全部优点并保持 ECSIM 的低成本（线性隐式场迭代 + 显式推进器）。障碍：ECSIM 的离散 Ampère 方程中，电荷守恒沉积 (44) 无法直接实施——时间步后半程的粒子轨迹未知，会迫使非线性求解。**解法：预测-校正两步方案**：猜测步 (X^n, V^n, e^n, b^n) ↦ (X^{n+1/2}, Ṽ^{n+1}, ẽ^{n+1}, b^{n+1})，再校正 ↦ (X^{n+1}, V^{n+1}, e^{n+1}, b^{n+1})。

[EN] 5.1 Guess step: mimics ECSIM with time-averages over the FIRST half time-step in the Lorentz force and current ⟨公式(59)-(63)：ṽ velocity kick with {Ẽ}^{n,−}; j̃^{n+1/2} = Σ q_k ṽ{Λ¹}^{n,−}⟩ — energy conservation untouched ⟨Proposition 5.1：E_h(Ṽ, ẽ, b) = E_h(V, e, b)⟩. The guess step decomposes as explicit pusher + linearly-implicit Maxwell system ⟨公式(70)⟩ with the time-averaged Lapenta matrix ⟨公式(72)(73)：L̄ⁿ with {Λ¹}^{n,−}⟩; always invertible ⟨Proposition 5.3⟩. Remark 5.4: using time averages (rather than point values) is not a necessity but gives a better guess field (smaller charge error) and the first-half averages are needed later anyway.

[中] 5.1 **猜测步**：仿 ECSIM，但洛伦兹力与电流改用**前半时间步**的时间平均 ⟨公式(59)-(63)⟩——能量守恒不受影响 ⟨**命题 5.1**：E_h(Ṽ, ẽ, b) = E_h(V, e, b)⟩。猜测步同样分解为显式推进器 + 线性隐式 Maxwell 系统 ⟨公式(70)⟩，其中 Lapenta 矩阵换为时间平均版本 ⟨公式(72)(73)⟩；恒可逆 ⟨**命题 5.3**⟩。注 5.4：用时间平均（而非点值）并非必需，但 (i) 猜测场的电荷误差更小；(ii) 后续完整校正本来就需要前半步平均，顺手使用更简洁。

[EN] 5.2 Correction step: the guess current decomposes as ⟨公式(75)：J̃ = J^{n,−} + J̃^{n,−}/2⟩ where J^{n,−} is charge-conserving (first-half average, 公式(76)) and the remainder J̃^{n,−} is not. The correction replaces the latter by a conserving second-half-step average current ⟨公式(78)：J^{n,+} = Σ q_k ṽ^{n+1}{Λ¹}^{n,+}⟩ ⟨公式(79)⟩, and updates the field ⟨公式(80)：M1(e^{n+1} − ẽ^{n+1}) = −(Δt/2ε0)(j^{n,+} − j̃^{n,−})⟩; the resulting final current equals the full-step time average ⟨J^{n+1/2} = (J^{n,−} + J^{n,+})/2 = (1/Δt)∫J_h dt⟩ — charge-conserving. Energy lost by the field update is restored by a global velocity rescaling v^{n+1}_k = λṽ^{n+1}_k with ⟨公式(81)(82)：λ = (1 − ε0(‖E‖² − ‖Ẽ‖²)/Σm_k|ṽ|²)^{1/2}⟩. Theorem 5.5: ChECSIM preserves the discrete Gauss laws (45)-(46) AND the discrete energy (48). Remark 5.6: unlike Marder's pseudo-current correction, the current correction is both **local and exactly charge-conserving**. Proposition 5.7 (feasibility): if ⟨公式(83)：2Δt‖γⁿ‖_m ≤ ‖vⁿ‖_m⟩ (γ acceleration terms) and the time-step satisfies a bound involving the field steepness ‖∇Ē‖_{1,∞} ⟨公式(84)(85)：Δt < Δt*⟩, the rescaling parameter is well-defined — otherwise a dynamic time-step adaptation suffices. Remark 5.8: global rescaling used for simplicity; a **local rescaling** λ_k ⟨公式(86)(87)，thermostat-inspired⟩ avoids rescaling particles where the current correction is small — investigated in the diode test-case.

[中] 5.2 **校正步**：猜测电流分解为 ⟨公式(75)：J̃ = J^{n,−} + J̃^{n,−}/2⟩——J^{n,−} 是电荷守恒的前半步平均电流 ⟨公式(76)⟩，其余项不守恒。校正把后者替换为**后半步平均的守恒电流** ⟨公式(78)(79)⟩，并更新场 ⟨公式(80)⟩——最终电流恰为全步时间平均 ⟨J^{n+1/2} = (J^{n,−} + J^{n,+})/2⟩，电荷守恒。场更新损失的能量由**全局速度重标定** v^{n+1}_k = λṽ^{n+1}_k 恢复 ⟨公式(81)(82)⟩。**定理 5.5：ChECSIM 同时保持离散 Gauss 定律 (45)(46) 与离散能量 (48)**。注 5.6：与 Marder 伪电流校正不同，此电流校正**既是局部的又是严格电荷守恒的**。**命题 5.7（可行性）**：若 ⟨公式(83)：2Δt‖γⁿ‖_m ≤ ‖vⁿ‖_m⟩（γ 为猜测步加速度项）且时间步满足含场陡峭度 ‖∇Ē‖_{1,∞} 的上界 ⟨公式(84)(85)⟩，则重标定参数 λ 良定义——否则动态调步即可。注 5.8：为简单起见用全局重标定；**局部重标定** λ_k ⟨公式(86)(87)，受恒温器方法启发⟩ 避免对电流校正很小的区域粒子做无谓重标定——在二极管算例中考察。

## 7. Conclusion｜第7章 结论

[EN] ECSIM was reformulated in a structure-preserving finite element setting, and this general discrete framework was used to complement the original energy-conserving method with an explicit, local charge-conserving correction step based on FEM-PIC conservative current deposition. The resulting ChECSIM scheme is still linearly implicit in the fields and explicit in the particles, and preserves exactly the total energy and the discrete charge compatible with the structure-preserving FEM framework. A condition for the correction step to be well-defined is that the kinetic energy exceeds the field correction energy; a rigorous time-step criterion guarantees this. In practice one may need to adapt the time-step dynamically, but this was not needed in the experiments. A locally adapted particle correction was proposed to avoid rescaling velocities in regions with little current error. The numerical experiments exhibit a clear improvement of stability, in particular for configurations with large time steps and systems not isolated from exterior energy sources.

[中] 本文把 ECSIM 重构于保结构有限元框架中，并利用该框架补上了一个**显式、局部的电荷守恒校正步**（基于 FEM-PIC 守恒电流沉积技术）。所得 ChECSIM 仍然"场线性隐式 + 粒子显式"，且在保结构有限元框架相容的意义下**精确保持总能量与离散电荷**。校正步良定义的条件是动能高于场校正能量；文中给出了严格的时间步判据保证之。实践中可能需要动态调步，但本文实验从未触发。还提出了**局部化粒子校正**以避免在电流误差小的区域重标定速度。数值实验清晰展示了新格式稳定性的改进——尤其是大时间步与"系统与外部能量源不隔离"的构型。
