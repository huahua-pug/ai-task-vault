# 基于Helmholtz-旋度混合公式系统的二维大地电磁有限元建模（英中对照·分片1：摘要、引言、三种公式系统）

体例：[EN] 原文（公式用字体安全 Unicode），[中] 汉译。

## Abstract｜摘要

[EN] The magnetotelluric (MT) method is an important geophysical electromagnetic induction exploration tool; with proper inversion routines we can image the electrical conductivity structures of the Earth. A reliable inversion needs to iteratively call forward modeling solvers. A novel hybrid Helmholtz-curl formulae system is presented for 2D MT finite element modeling. In the air space with small conductivities, the well-behaved Helmholtz equation represents the field propagation; in the Earth domain with positive conductivities, the well-behaved curl-curl equation represents the inductive and propagating behaviors. On the air-Earth interface with arbitrary topographies, continuity conditions of the EM fields guarantee uniqueness. Synthetic models with simple and complex geo-electrical structures incorporating topographies verify the system: numerically stable with low air conductivity values and low signal frequencies — an alternative algorithm for 2D MT forward modeling.

[中] 大地电磁（MT）法是重要的地球物理电磁感应勘探工具；配合适当的反演流程可成像地球电导率结构。可靠的反演需反复调用正演求解器。本文提出一种新颖的 **Helmholtz-旋度混合公式系统**用于二维 MT 有限元正演：在电导率极小的**空气域**，用性质良好的 Helmholtz 方程描述场传播；在电导率为正的**大地域**，用性质良好的旋度-旋度（curl-curl）方程描述场的感应与传播行为；在带任意地形起伏的**空气-大地界面**上，强加电磁场连续性条件以保证解的唯一性。含简单与复杂地电结构（带地形）的多个合成模型验证表明：该混合系统在低空气电导率、低信号频率下数值稳定——是二维 MT 正演的一种替代算法。

## 1. Introduction｜引言

[EN] MT images deep conductivity structures using primary time-harmonic fields from ionospheric currents; induced currents and interface charges generate secondary fields; conductivity information is extracted from the total fields. Applications: deep mineral exploration, geothermal, oil & gas, crust imaging. The Earth has 2D background structures (plate tectonics, subduction, mountain chains) — 2D MT imaging resolves them efficiently and provides initial/reference models for 3D MT imaging; 2D MT inversion is irreplaceable. Software: WinGLink, MARE2DEM, self-made codes — the core engine is the forward solver. Closed-form solutions exist only for few simple models; numerical methods: IE (requires closed-form Green's functions of the background — simple cases only), FD, FV, FE.

[中] MT 法利用电离层随机电流产生的时谐一次场穿透地下结构；导电体内形成感应电流产生二次场，电导率突变界面上电荷积累产生二次电场；从总场中提取电导率信息。应用：深部矿产、地热、油气、地壳成像。地球存在**二维背景构造**（板块构造、俯冲、造山带）——二维 MT 成像能高效分辨之，并作为三维 MT 成像的可靠初始/参考模型；二维 MT 反演不可替代。软件：WinGLink、MARE2DEM 与自研代码——核心是正演引擎。只有少数简单模型有闭式解；数值方法：积分方程（要求背景格林函数闭式——只限简单情形）、有限差分、有限体积、有限元。

[EN] A common feature of existing 2D MT routines: the Helmholtz equation is adopted in BOTH air and Earth domains ("Helmholtz formula system"): TE/TM decoupling on 2D structures; primary variables Ex and Hx solved by scalar second-order Helmholtz equations; auxiliary variables (Ey, Ez, Hy, Hz) computed by numerical differentiation of the primaries — accuracy loss. Another popular governing equation is the curl-curl electric/magnetic field equation, widely used only in 3D; we also present its 2D application ("Curl formula system"), which indirectly solves the auxiliary variables. In this study: a novel algorithm treating air and Earth separately, solving ALL primary and auxiliary components simultaneously — Helmholtz equations for the primary variables in the air space (homogeneous, tiny/zero conductivity), curl-curl equations for the auxiliary variables in the Earth domain (positive conductivity → well-posed), continuity conditions on the air-Earth interface, 1D layered analytic sources on outer boundaries: the "air-Earth decomposition" or "Helmholtz-curl formulae system". Prior air-Earth decompositions [52-54] used potentials (needing numerical differentiation → accuracy decreases); the present one is field-based.

[中] 现有二维 MT 正演的共性：**空气与大地全域都用 Helmholtz 方程**（"Helmholtz 公式系统"）：MT 入射场沿走向不变，总场在二维构造上解耦为 TE/TM 两模式；主变量 Ex、Hx 用标量二阶 Helmholtz 方程求解；辅助变量（Ey、Ez、Hy、Hz）由主变量**数值求导**得到——存在精度损失。另一类常用方程是电场/磁场 curl-curl 方程，但几乎只在三维使用；我们也给出其二维应用（"Curl 公式系统"）——它间接求解的是辅助变量。本文新算法：**空-地分别处理、同时求解全部主变量与辅助变量**——空气中（均匀、电导率极小/零）用主变量的 Helmholtz 方程，大地域（电导率恒正）用辅助变量的 curl-curl 方程，空气-大地界面强加连续性条件，外边界用一维层状模型解析源场：称为"空-地分解算法"或"Helmholtz-curl 公式系统"。此前的空-地分解 [52-54] 多用势函数作变量（仍需数值微分 → 精度下降）；本文是**基于场**的方案。

## 2. Three formulae systems｜三种公式系统

[EN] Domain: air Ω₁₂ (homogeneous σ₂) + Earth Ω₁₁ (heterogeneous σ₁(y,z)); time factor e^{−iωt}; Maxwell ⟨公式(1)(2)：∇×E = iωμH；∇×H = σE⟩. 2D expansion (x = strike) ⟨公式(3)-(8)⟩. (1) Helmholtz system: primary variables Ex (TE: α=1, β=iωμσ) and Hx (TM: α=1/σ, β=iωμ) via ⟨公式(9)：∇·α∇u + βu = 0⟩ in BOTH air and Earth; linear node elements [50]; aux variables by differentiation ⟨公式(4)(5)(7)(8)⟩. For TM, Hx is constant in the air (excluded there in implementations). (2) Curl system: auxiliary variables H = (0,Hy,Hz) (TE: α=1/σ, β=−iωμ) and E = (0,Ey,Ez) (TM: α=1, β=−iωμσ) via ⟨公式(12)：∇×α∇×u + βu = 0⟩ in BOTH domains; linear edge elements ⟨公式(15)-(17)⟩ with boundary terms f = −n̂×E0 (TE) / −iωμ n̂×H0 (TM) from 1D layered analytics; primaries recovered by differentiation ⟨公式(3)(6)⟩.

[中] 计算域：空气 Ω₁₂（均匀 σ₂）+ 大地 Ω₁₁（非均匀 σ₁(y,z)）；时谐因子 e^{−iωt}；Maxwell 方程 ⟨公式(1)(2)⟩；二维展开（x 为走向）⟨公式(3)-(8)⟩。(1) **Helmholtz 公式系统**：主变量 Ex（TE：α=1、β=iωμσ）与 Hx（TM：α=1/σ、β=iωμ）经 ⟨公式(9)⟩ 在空气与大地全域求解；线性节点元；辅助变量靠求导 ⟨公式(4)(5)(7)(8)⟩。TM 模式下空气内 Hx 为常数（实现中常不含空气层）。(2) **Curl 公式系统**：辅助变量 H = (0,Hy,Hz)（TE：α=1/σ、β=−iωμ）与 E = (0,Ey,Ez)（TM：α=1、β=−iωμσ）经 ⟨公式(12)⟩ 在全域求解；线性棱边元离散 ⟨公式(15)-(17)⟩，边界项 f 由一维层状解析场给出；主变量再由求导恢复 ⟨公式(3)(6)⟩。

[EN] (3) Helmholtz-curl formulae system (air-Earth decomposition): air → Helmholtz ⟨公式(18)(19)⟩; Earth (positive σ) → curl-curl; continuity conditions on the air-Earth interface (arbitrary topography) exchange scattered fields; 1D layered closed-form sources on ∂Ω guarantee uniqueness. Four possible hybrid combinations; for 2D MT: TE mode — air variable Ex, Earth variable H = (0,Hy,Hz); TM — air Hx, Earth E = (0,Ey,Ez) ⟨公式(20)-(23)⟩. The system simultaneously solves all primary and auxiliary components — no numerical differentiation (vs potential-based [52-54]). Weak forms: multiply (20) by vector test functions T ∈ H(curl,Ω₁₁) and (21) by scalar t ∈ H¹(Ω₁₂), integration by parts ⟨公式(24)(25)⟩; unified form ⟨公式(26)(27)⟩ with mode-dependent coefficients ⟨公式(28)(29)⟩ (source fields from 1D layered analytics). FE discretization: Triangle meshing; lowest-order edge functions N_j for the Earth vector field ⟨公式(30)⟩, linear node functions φ_l for the air scalar field ⟨公式(31)⟩; block linear system ⟨公式(34)-(40)：[A11 A12; A21 A22][X1;X2] = [B1;B2]⟩ — A11 sparse symmetric (Earth curl-curl), A12/A21 interface couplings, A22 sparse symmetric (air Helmholtz).

[中] (3) **Helmholtz-curl 公式系统（空-地分解）**：空气域 → Helmholtz 方程 ⟨公式(18)(19)⟩；大地域（σ>0）→ curl-curl 方程；空气-大地界面（任意地形）上的连续性条件交换散射场信息；∂Ω 上用一维层状闭式源场保证唯一性。可有四种组合；对二维 MT：TE 模式——空气变量 Ex、大地变量 H = (0,Hy,Hz)；TM——空气 Hx、大地 E = (0,Ey,Ez) ⟨公式(20)-(23)⟩。该系统**同时解出全部主变量与辅助变量**——无需数值微分（对比基于势的 [52-54]）。弱形式：对 (20) 乘矢量检验函数 T ∈ H(curl,Ω₁₁)、对 (21) 乘标量 t ∈ H¹(Ω₁₂)，分部积分 ⟨公式(24)(25)⟩；统一形式 ⟨公式(26)(27)⟩，模式相关系数 ⟨公式(28)(29)⟩（源场由一维层状解析式计算）。有限元离散：Triangle 剖分；大地矢量场用最低阶棱边函数 N_j ⟨公式(30)⟩、空气标量场用线性节点函数 φ_l ⟨公式(31)⟩；块线性系统 ⟨公式(34)-(40)：[A11 A12; A21 A22][X1;X2] = [B1;B2]⟩——A11 为大地 curl-curl 稀疏对称阵，A12/A21 为界面耦合阵，A22 为空气 Helmholtz 稀疏对称阵。

[EN] 2.4 MT response functions: apparent resistivity and phase from the impedance ⟨公式(41)-(43)：Z_ij = E_i/H_j；ρ_ij = |Z_ij|²/(ωμ)；φ_ij = arg(Z_ij)⟩ — extracted directly at air-Earth interface stations without differentiation.

[中] 2.4 MT 响应函数：感兴趣的量是视电阻率与相位，由阻抗导出 ⟨公式(41)-(43)⟩——直接在空气-大地界面测点上提取、无需求导。
