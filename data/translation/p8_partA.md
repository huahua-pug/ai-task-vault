# 瞬态电磁学的部分交错间断伽辽金方法（英中对照·分片1：摘要、引言、格式、子域构造）

体例：[EN] 原文（公式用字体安全 Unicode），[中] 汉译。

## Abstract｜摘要

[EN] Conservation of energy, spurious-free, and optimal convergence are among the most favorite ingredients of a DG-FETD method for transient electromagnetics. Unfortunately, due to reliance on dissipative upwind flux to suppress spurious modes, most existing DG-FETDs possess only one or two of them. We present a novel 2D DG-FETD combining all three. Based on E and H with approximation spaces composed of Whitney's edge functions, to inhibit spurious modes while keeping energy conservation, the dissipative numerical flux is not adopted: a carefully designed interface condition with non-dissipative central flux is employed — across each interior face, if E is strongly enforced tangentially continuous then H is weakly imposed discontinuous, and vice versa. Two partially staggered sets of subdomains are built for E and H via refining each cell into four similar elements. The system is advanced with second-order leap-frog. Examples demonstrate robustness and superiority over conventional DG-FETD.

[中] 能量守恒、无伪模（spurious-free）与最优收敛是瞬态电磁 DG-FETD 方法最受期待的三个性质。遗憾的是，由于依赖耗散性的迎风数值通量压制伪模，现有 DG-FETD 大多只具其一其二。本文提出一种兼具三者优点的新型二维 DG-FETD：以 E、H 为变量，逼近空间由 Whitney 棱边函数构成；为在保持能量守恒的同时抑制伪模，**不采用耗散性数值通量**，而是用精心设计的界面条件配合无耗散的中心通量——在除计算域边界外的每个面上，若 E 被强加切向连续，则 H 被弱施加为不连续，反之亦然。为满足该要求且不牺牲 DG 固有的并行性，为 E、H 分别构造**部分交错的两组子域**（把初始网格每单元细分为四个相似子单元生成）。最终离散系统用二阶 leap-frog 推进。算例表明方法稳健、显著优于常规 DG-FETD。

## 1. Introduction｜引言

[EN] DG-FETD combines merits of FDTD, FVTD, FETD: inherent parallelism with explicit integrators, flexibility for complex geometries, hp-adaptability, different approximation spaces per element. All advantages come from the numerical flux communicating fields between adjacent subdomains — conservation, convergence, spectrum characteristics strongly depend on it. Two fluxes: central flux (CF, non-dissipative → conservative energy but polluted spectrum: spurious modes intermingle with physical solutions) and upwind flux (UF, dissipative → clear spectrum but not energy conservative, usually higher accuracy and optimal convergence). No method with both conservative energy and clear spectrum was known — yet it is exactly what CEM community expects (plasma and resonance simulations).

[中] DG-FETD 集合了 FDTD、FVTD、FETD 的优点：显式时间积分的天然并行性、复杂几何适应性、hp 自适应、逐单元不同逼近空间。这些优点都来自**数值通量**——能量守恒、收敛率、谱特性强烈依赖通量选择。两类通量性质迥异：**中心通量 CF** 无耗散 → 能量守恒但谱被污染（伪模与物理解混叠）；**迎风通量 UF** 有耗散 → 谱干净但不守恒能量（通常精度更高、最优收敛 δ^{n+1}）。似乎无论 CF 还是 UF，都无法同时做到能量守恒与谱干净——而这正是计算电磁学界（等离子体与谐振问题模拟）真正期待的。

[EN] Prior attempts: Hesthaven's asymmetric dispersive penalty (non-dissipative but suboptimal, one order lower; unclear why penalty cannot be imposed on both E and H); Liu et al. hybrid CG-DG with Whitney base (E and B; subdomains with continuous FETD inside, CF on interfaces; leapfrog; first energy-conserving spurious-free DG-FETD — but no criterion on subdomain size; one-element subdomains fail; optimality unproven); Liu et al. E/B with interpolation spaces differing by one order (CF + symplectic = conservative; no theoretical foundation for why different spaces; accuracy dominated by lower order → costly memory); Chung et al. staggered DG for 2D Maxwell (preserves conservation properties, automatic discrete divergence-free — the only irregular-mesh DG-FETD with all three advantages; but staggered grids via barycenter refinement give poor mesh quality, special local basis functions hard to extend to higher dimensions).

[中] 已有尝试：Hesthaven 的非对称色散惩罚项（无耗散但收敛次优、低一阶，且为何不能同时对 E、H 施加惩罚不明）；Liu 等的 CG-DG 混合方法（E 与 B 为变量、子域内连续 FETD + 界面 CF + leapfrog——首个能量守恒且无伪模的 DG-FETD，但**子域规模无准则**，据作者经验单元素子域无法消除伪模，最优性也无证据）；Liu 等基于 E/B、插值空间错开一阶的格式（CF+辛积分=守恒，但为何 E、H 要用不同空间缺理论解释，精度被低阶空间主导、内存代价大）；Chung 等的交错 DG（保持守恒性质、自动满足离散无散——**已知唯一兼具三优的不规则网格 DG-FETD**；但其交错网格用重心细分、网格质量差，需特殊局部基函数补救，难以推广到高维）。

[EN] Key insight: the discrepancy in tangential continuity of the basis functions for E and H on interior edges is what makes staggered DG spurious-free with central flux. Proposed method: enforce that across each interior face, if E is tangentially continuous then H is discontinuous, and vice versa (FDTD, classical EB continuous FETD, and Chung's method all satisfy this condition — ours is more general). Two partially staggered subdomain sets built for E and H (refining each cell into four similar elements — no mesh-quality loss). Unlike Chung, approximation spaces are Whitney edge functions (Nédélec first family) — well established in 3D, no barrier to higher dimensions. Second-order leapfrog. Outline: Section 2 formulism; space discretization; Section 4 convergence/spectrum/stability; examples; Section 6 remarks and conclusions.

[中] **关键洞察**：交错 DG 在中心通量下无伪模的原因，很可能是 E 与 H 基函数在内部边上切向连续性的**错位**。据此提出新方法：每个内部面上，若 E 基函数切向连续则 H 不连续，反之亦然（FDTD、经典 EB 连续 FETD、Chung 方法都满足此条件——本方法是其推广）。为满足该条件，为 E、H 构造**部分交错的两组子域**（每单元四相似细分，网格质量无损）。与 Chung 不同，逼近空间用 Whitney 棱边函数（Nédélec 第一族）——三维理论成熟，向高维推广无障碍。时间用二阶 leap-frog。

## 2. Formulism｜格式

[EN] 2D TE mode first-order curl equations with E_t, H_z ⟨公式(1)(2)：ε∂E_t/∂t = ∇_t×H_z − J_imp − σE_t；μ∂H_z/∂t = −∇_t×E_t⟩. Element-wise expansion: E_t with Whitney functions W_i (⟨W_i = λ_i1∇λ_i2 − λ_i2∇λ_i1⟩), H_z with barycentric coordinates λ_j. 2.1 Continuous Galerkin: weak forms ⟨公式(3)(4)⟩ → element matrices ⟨公式(5)(6)⟩ → global assembly ⟨公式(7)(8)⟩: tangential continuity of both E and H enforced → large linear system per time step (expensive) and polluted spectrum. 2.2 Discontinuous Galerkin: strong forms with numerical fluxes E*, H* ⟨公式(9)(10)⟩; fluxes ⟨Y_kE_k + Y_k'E_k')/(Y_k+Y_k') − ϑ n×(H_k − H_k') etc.⟩ with ϑ ∈ [0,1] (ϑ=0 central, ϑ=1 upwind); element-wise update ⟨公式(11)(12)⟩ with interface matrices F. 2.3 Partially staggered DG: two independent complete-coverage subdomain sets S^e_p (P subdomains) for E and S^h_q (Q subdomains) for H; within each E-subdomain E is tangential continuous (one DOF per interior edge) while H is discontinuous (n DOFs at an n-triangle vertex); on subdomain borders the roles swap ⟨公式(13)(14)⟩. Mass matrices block-diagonal by subdomain → each subdomain advances independently with leapfrog ⟨algorithm⟩ — parallelism preserved.

[中] 2D TE 模式一阶旋度方程，变量 E_t、H_z ⟨公式(1)(2)⟩。单元内展开：E_t 用 Whitney 函数 W_i，H_z 用重心坐标 λ_j。2.1 连续 Galerkin：弱形式 ⟨公式(3)(4)⟩ → 单元矩阵 ⟨公式(5)(6)⟩ → 全局组装 ⟨公式(7)(8)⟩：E 与 H 的切向连续同时强加 → 每步需解大型线性系统（昂贵）且谱被污染。2.2 间断 Galerkin：含数值通量 E*、H* 的强形式 ⟨公式(9)(10)⟩；通量公式（阻抗加权平均 − ϑ n×(H_k − H_k')，ϑ ∈ [0,1]：ϑ=0 中心通量、ϑ=1 迎风通量）；逐单元更新 ⟨公式(11)(12)⟩。2.3 **部分交错 DG**：为 E、H 各建一组完全覆盖计算域的独立子域（E 的 P 个、H 的 Q 个）；在每个 E-子域内部 E 切向连续（内部边一个自由度）而 H 不连续（n 个三角形共享的顶点有 n 个磁自由度）；子域边界上角色互换 ⟨公式(13)(14)⟩。质量阵按子域块对角 → 显式 leapfrog 时**各子域可独立推进**（算法伪代码给出）——并行性得以保留。

## 3. Construction of subdomains｜子域构造

[EN] Initial triangulation (N_e elements, N_v vertices) refined one level by connecting edge midpoints → four similar sub-elements per triangle (no quality loss; mesh "M/4"). Set A: the four sub-elements within each initial element form one subdomain → N_e subdomains. Set B: sub-elements sharing each initial vertex form one subdomain; the remaining mid-edge sub-elements (no common edges) each form an independent subdomain → N_v + N_e subdomains. (4E, XH): set A for E, set B for H; (XE, 4H): reversed. Comparison with Chung's method ("M/3": barycenter-to-vertices refinement, sub-elements much lower quality; sets (3E, XH)/(XE, 3H)).

[中] 初始三角剖分（N_e 单元、N_v 顶点）按**边中点细分**一层 → 每三角 4 个相似子单元（网格质量无损，记 "M/4"）。**集合 A**：每个初始单元内的 4 个子单元构成一个子域 → 共 N_e 个。**集合 B**：共享初始顶点的子单元构成一个子域；其余以边中点为顶点、彼此无公共边的子单元各自独立成域 → 共 N_v + N_e 个。(4E, XH)：A 用于 E、B 用于 H；(XE, 4H)：反之。与 Chung 方法对比（"M/3"：重心-顶点细分、子单元质量显著下降；对应 (3E, XH)/(XE, 3H) 两套配置）。
