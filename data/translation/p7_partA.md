# 时域电磁的异步时空间断伽辽金有限元方法（英中对照·分片1：摘要、引言、微分形式表述、黎曼目标通量）

体例：[EN] 原文（公式用字体安全 Unicode；微分形式记号 d、∧、⋆ 保留），[中] 汉译。

## Abstract｜摘要

[EN] We present an asynchronous spacetime discontinuous Galerkin (aSDG) method for time domain electromagnetics in which space and time are directly discretized. By using differential forms we express Maxwell's equations, and consequently their DG discretization, for arbitrary domains in spacetime. Elements are discretized with electric and magnetic basis functions discontinuous across all inter-element boundaries, with arbitrary high and per-element spacetime orders. On unstructured grids satisfying a specific causality constraint the method has a local, asynchronous solution procedure with linear complexity in the number of elements. Convergence is studied on 1D–3D uniform grids for energy dissipation, error versus exact solution, and von Neumann dissipation/dispersion errors; 2D simulations demonstrate resolution of sharp wave fronts.

[中] 本文提出面向时域电磁学的**异步时空间断伽辽金（aSDG）**有限元方法，空间与时间被直接离散。借助**微分形式**，Maxwell 方程组及其 DG 离散可在时空任意域上表达。单元用跨一切单元边界均不连续的电、磁基函数离散，时空阶数可任意高且**逐单元可调**。当非结构网格满足特定因果性约束时，方法具有局部、异步的求解流程，复杂度随单元数线性增长。在 1D–3D 均匀网格上数值考察能量耗散、相对精确解的误差以及 von Neumann 耗散/色散误差的收敛性；二维算例展示了对尖锐波前的分辨能力。

## 1. Introduction｜引言

[EN] TD vs FD: TD handles material nonlinearities (metamaterials) more naturally; one broadband TD run replaces many FD analyses; causality gives locality — TD solvers exploiting causality scale O(N) whereas FD-FEM scales O(N²)+; early unsteady relaxation needs direct TD. TDDG in CEM is recent; most TDDG use explicit time integration (Runge–Kutta or leap-frog) due to block-diagonal matrices and linear cost. The aSDG method (prior: elastodynamics, hyperbolic heat conduction, conservation laws/Euler, advection–diffusion) directly discretizes spacetime with unstructured causal grids: arbitrarily high temporal order, adjustable per element; unlike implicit spacetime DG [27-30] (global synchronization), causality relaxes synchronicity → linear cost. Compared with Tent-Pitcher-type advances [34,35] and Monk–Richter [36]: aSDG meshing can produce non-vertical interior faces (requiring target flux definitions), and richer mesh-adaptive vocabulary (error indicators [35,37], moving interfaces like crack surfaces in spacetime [38]) — differential forms handle these complex geometries without spacetime normal vectors.

[中] 时域（TD）与频域（FD）对比：TD 更自然地处理材料非线性（超材料等）；一次宽带 TD 计算等价于多个频点的 FD 分析；**因果性带来局部性**——充分利用因果性的 TD 求解器复杂度 O(N)，而 FD-FEM 为 O(N²) 以上；早期非平稳弛豫过程必须直接 TD。TDDG 进入 CEM 较晚，多数采用显式时间积分（Runge–Kutta 或 leap-frog）。aSDG 方法（此前已用于线弹性动力学、双曲热传导、守恒律/欧拉方程、对流扩散）用满足**因果性约束**的非结构网格直接离散时空：时间阶可任意高且逐单元可调；相比隐式时空 DG [27-30] 的全局同步，因果网格解除同步约束、复杂度线性。与 Tent-Pitcher 类推进 [34,35] 及 Monk–Richter [36] 相比：aSDG 的网格操作可产生非竖直内侧面（需定义目标通量），且自适应网格操作需要更丰富的时空网格操作词表（误差指标 [35,37]、时空裂纹面等运动界面跟踪 [38]）——微分形式表述无需定义时空法向量即可处理这些复杂几何。

[EN] The synchronization constraint plagues domains with diverse element sizes and mesh adaptivity; IMEX and local time stepping (LTS) only partially remedy it. ADER DG [39] fully removes global time stepping via asynchronous local stepping and achieves element-level arbitrary temporal order via Taylor expansion (Cauchy–Kovalewski procedure). aSDG shares these properties but eliminates the fairly complex Cauchy–Kovalewski algorithm by direct spacetime discretization; unstructured spacetime elements also enable powerful mesh adaptation for tracking strong discontinuities (not pursued herein). Organization: §2 Maxwell balance laws in differential forms for arbitrary spacetime domains (+ PDEs and jumps); §3 weak enforcement in aSDG; §4 error indicators; §5 implementation; §6 convergence for smooth/nonsmooth fields and 2D sharp fronts.

[中] 全局时间步同步约束是"单元尺寸差异大"域模拟与网格自适应的主要障碍；IMEX 与局部时间步进（LTS）只能部分缓解。ADER-DG [39] 用异步局部时间步进彻底解除全局步进约束，并借 Taylor 展开（Cauchy–Kovalewski 程序）实现逐单元任意时间阶。aSDG 同样具备这些性质，但**直接时空离散免除了相当复杂的 Cauchy–Kovalewski 程序**；非结构时空单元还为跟踪强间断提供了更强的网格自适应能力（本文未展开）。结构：§2 时空任意域上的 Maxwell 平衡律微分形式（含 PDE 与跳跃条件）；§3 aSDG 弱强制；§4 误差指标；§5 实现；§6 光滑/非光滑场收敛性与二维尖锐波前。
