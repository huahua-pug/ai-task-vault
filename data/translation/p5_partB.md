# 英中对照·分片2：基准测试（收敛性、孤子、电磁激波管、ICF组分分离）与结论

## 4. Benchmarks and performance｜基准测试与性能

[EN] 4.1 Linear advection convergence: fixed Δt isolates spatial convergence — CG and DG of the same order converge at the same rate; the CG L2-error plateaus around 10⁻⁵ due to artificial dissipation (higher order needs smaller κ). Fixed CFL isolates temporal convergence — both implicit CG (Crank–Nicolson) and explicit DG (TVB-RK2) are second-order accurate.

[中] 4.1 线性对流收敛试验：固定 Δt 分离空间收敛——同阶 CG 与 DG 收敛率相同；CG 的 L2 误差因人工耗散在 10⁻⁵ 附近进入平台（阶数越高所需 κ 越小）。固定 CFL 分离时间收敛——隐式 CG（Crank–Nicolson）与显式 DG（TVB-RK2）均为二阶时间精度。

[EN] 4.2 Plasma soliton (two-fluid, smooth): 512 second-order elements, periodic; artificial parameters (mass ratio, c/csi) are commonly reduced to relax time-step limits — Table 1 compares BFEM vs explicit DG cost: DG faster for mass ratio 25; as the mass ratio grows (electron mass decreases → electron plasma frequency rises) DG time grows while BFEM stays flat; the light-speed ratio matters even more: at realistic values (mi/me = 1836, c/csi = 1000/√2) BFEM is −48.14% cheaper. Solution comparison against a converged FV (5000 cells) solution: DG shows strong dispersion at realistic mass ratio ("not well understood at this point"); BFEM peaks higher (less dissipative) but exhibits phase error because the electron plasma frequency is not resolved; BFEM L2 relative error ≈ 1.5% with only 10% of the converged solution's resolution. With mass ratio 1 both methods resolve plasma frequencies and errors are comparable.

[中] 4.2 等离子体孤子（双流体、光滑解）：512 个二阶单元、周期边界。等离子体模拟中常人为减小离子/电子质量比与 c/csi 以放松时间步限制——表 1 对比 BFEM 与显式 DG 的成本：质量比 25 时 DG 明显更快；随质量比增大（电子质量减小→电子等离子体频率升高）DG 耗时增长而 BFEM 基本不变；光速比影响更大：真实参数（mi/me = 1836，c/csi = 1000/√2）下 BFEM 便宜 48.14%。与收敛 FV 解（5000 单元）对比：真实质量比下 DG 出现强色散（"原因尚不清楚，需进一步研究"）；BFEM 峰值更高（耗散更小）但因未分辨电子等离子体频率而存在**相位误差**（解相对收敛解空间平移）；BFEM 的 L2 相对误差约 1.5%，而其分辨率仅为收敛解的 10%。质量比为 1 时两法误差相当（都分辨了等离子体频率）。

[EN] 4.3 Electromagnetic plasma shock tube (two-fluid, mi/me = 1836, c/csi = 110, r_L = 0.73): solution features FR–SC–CD–SS–FR plus fast EM waves (whistler) faster than MHD waves. Table 2 lists characteristic speeds/frequencies; the explicit time-step limit ⟨公式(52)：Δt_max = min(Δx/cse, Δx/csi, Δx/c, β/ωce, β/ωci, β/ωpe, β/ωpi)⟩ is set by the electron plasma frequency (Δt_max = 2.33×10⁻⁴); BFEM implicit steps reach 42.9·Δt_max (limited by ion plasma frequency). The implicit solution resolves all main features except the fast rarefaction wave and fast EM waves; the slow compound wave is better resolved (ion dynamics). 4.3.2 Artificial dissipation: κ too small → node-to-node oscillations grow, Newton stiffer; κ too big → physics smoothed. Separate κ_e (electron) and κ_EM (Maxwell): reducing κ_e to 10⁻⁷ sharpens the compound wave; reducing κ_EM from 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻⁵ (κ_e fixed 10⁻⁶) recovers the fast EM oscillations and the right fast rarefaction — remarkable agreement with DG. Systematic/dynamic κ selection is left as improvement.

[中] 4.3 电磁等离子体激波管（双流体，mi/me = 1836，c/csi = 110，r_L = 0.73）：解结构为快稀疏波-慢复合波-接触间断-慢激波-快稀疏波，外加快于 MHD 波的快电磁波（含哨声波）。表 2 列出特征速度/频率；显式时间步上限 ⟨公式(52)⟩ 由电子等离子体频率决定（Δt_max = 2.33×10⁻⁴）；BFEM 隐式步长可达 **42.9 倍 Δt_max**（受离子等离子体频率限制）。隐式解分辨了除快稀疏波与快电磁波外的全部主要特征；慢复合波（离子动力学）分辨得更好。4.3.2 人工耗散：κ 太小 → 节点间振荡增长、Newton 更刚；κ 太大 → 物理特征被抹平。电子与 Maxwell 方程可分别取 κ_e 与 κ_EM：κ_e 降至 10⁻⁷ 使复合波更清晰；κ_EM 从 10⁻⁴ 降至 10⁻⁵（κ_e 固定 10⁻⁶）恢复快电磁波振荡与右侧快稀疏波——与 DG 解惊人一致。系统性/动态 κ 选取留作改进。

[EN] 4.4 Species separation in ICF capsule implosions: three fluids (deuterium, heavy ion μ = 10 as tritium surrogate, electrons); realistic c/csp = 3065, mass ratio 1836; discontinuous initial data; electron CFL 1 vs 20: species separation identical, post-shock oscillations differ; CPU cost drops with electron CFL up to 10, error increases but remains small.

[中] 4.4 ICF 靶丸内爆中的组分分离：三流体（氘、重离子 μ = 10 作为氚替代、电子）；真实参数 c/csp = 3065、质量比 1836；间断初值。电子 CFL 数 1 与 20 对比：**组分分离结果相同**，激波后振荡不同；CPU 成本随电子 CFL 数增大而下降（CFL > 10 后收益递减），误差增大但仍小。

## 5. Conclusion｜第5章 结论

[EN] BFEM: DG + explicit RK for ion/neutral fluids; CG + implicit Crank–Nicolson for electron fluid and EM fields. DG captures shocks/discontinuities; CG computes smooth solutions efficiently and robustly. Each component independently verified at expected orders. Soliton cost study: DG cheaper for unphysical parameter values; BFEM cheaper for realistic mass ratio and light speed — considerable savings for fusion plasmas (mass ratio 3672 for D-T, more for non-hydrogen). EM shock: implicit time step 42.9× the explicit limit; fast EM waves and fast rarefaction not resolved, but shock location, contact discontinuity and compound wave accurately computed; artificial dissipation tuned per equation system; reducing κ_EM by ten gives remarkable agreement with DG. ICF application: stable solutions capturing overall ion structures at significantly lower cost. Potential: problems where slow dynamics are of primary interest but fast dynamics still matter. Improvements: dynamically adjusting artificial dissipation; extension to multiple dimensions straightforward (savings hold since they come from implicit fast dynamics; per-step DG is cheaper but needs many more steps).

[中] BFEM：离子/中性流体用 DG + 显式 RK；电子流体与电磁场用 CG + 隐式 Crank–Nicolson。DG 精确捕捉激波与间断；CG 高效稳健地计算光滑解。各组件独立验证达到预期精度阶。孤子成本研究：非物理参数下 DG 更快；真实质量比与光速下 BFEM 更省——对聚变等离子体（D-T 质量比 3672、非氢等离子体更大）节省可观。电磁激波：隐式步长为显式上限的 42.9 倍；快电磁波与快稀疏波未被分辨，但激波位置、接触间断与复合波计算准确；人工耗散按方程组分别调节；κ_EM 降低十倍后与 DG 解惊人一致。ICF 应用：以显著更低的成本得到捕捉整体离子结构的稳定解。适用前景：慢动力学为主、但快动力学仍起作用的多尺度问题。改进方向：动态调节人工耗散；多维扩展直接（节省来自隐式处理快动力学，多维依然成立；DG 单步更便宜但步数多得多）。
